The '''503<sup>rd</sup> Joint Fighter Wing "Typhoon Witches"''' (第503統合戦闘航空団 「タイフーンウィッチーズ」) are based in [[Wikipedia:Chelyabinsk|Chelyabinsk]], [[Orussia]]. Their main objective is the defense and recovery of [[Orussia]] and [[Karlsland]].

Their HQ, Chelyabinsk, has oil, coal pits, and other resources, and the city itself has a lot of heavy industry. After the increase of Neuroi attacks in recent years, most of Eurasia's factories (including weapon & ammo factories) were relocated here, and as a result the city has become an important industrial city in [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurasia Eurasia].

To the south of Chelyabinsk, [[Wikipedia:Magnitogorsk|Magnitogorsk]] has vast deposits of iron ore and a large steel industry, while in the west, [[Wikipedia:Ufa|Ufa]] has oil refineries. A defense line is currently in place to cover both cities. [[Wikipedia:Samara, Russia|Samara]], located along the east of the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volga_River Volga], has become the frontline HQ for the 503<sup>rd</sup> Joint Fighter Wing.
