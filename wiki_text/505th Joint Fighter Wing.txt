The '''505<sup>th</sup> Joint Fighter Wing "Mirage Witches"''' (第505統合戦闘航空団「ミラージュウィッチーズ」) are based at [[Wikipedia:Carpathian Mountains|Carpathian Mountains]] in the southern part of [[Ostmark]]. Their main objective is a border surveillance mission around the Carpathian Mountains. They have been retreating from time to time again. After the fall of Ostmark the 505<sup>th</sup> Joint Fighter Wing's headquarters often changed ([[Wikipedia:Volgograd|Tsaritsyn]], [[Wikipedia:Rostov-on-Don|Rostov]], [[Wikipedia:Kharkiv|Kharkiv]] and finally south across the [[Wikipedia:Caspian Sea|Caspian Sea]] to the [[Wikipedia:Absheron peninsula|Absheron peninsula]]). They then later received support from [[Fuso]] and were able to regroup themselves to face against the [[Neuroi]]. The reason they were being called the "Mirage Witches" is because of how good they were in retreating battles. It is also said that [[Kuroe Ayaka]] had temporarily being stationed in this team for quite some time before being transferred to another unit.
