'''The 507th Joint Fighter Wing''' (第507統合戦闘航空団「サイレントウィッチーズ」) is a volunteer group previously called the ''Suomus Independent Volunteer Aerial Squadron''. To many, they bear the nickname "Silent Witches" or, less flatteringly, the "Suomus Misfit Squadron" in reference to the reputation of some squad members. As per the name, it is stationed in [[Suomus]].

==Function==
Established originally for the protection of Suomus, the 507th was previously known as the Suomus Independent Volunteer Air Squadron prior to being established as a Joint Fighter Wing. In commemoration of those great efforts, as they couldn't reach out on the scale that was necessary, they were given the digits 507 to raise their status as a Joint Fighter Wing. Shortly after, [[Elma Leivonen]] retired and left the new Joint Fighter Wing in the hands of [[Sakomizu Haruka]]. Since the witches from the Suomus Misfits Squadron had either retired or were dispatched elsewhere, new witches were dispatched to Suomus by signatory nations.

==Service history==
They are confirmed to be deployed early in the war against the [[Neuroi]] in 1939, stationed in [[Kauhava]] Airbase.
