'''Aira Paivikki Linnamaa''' (アイラ・ペイヴィッキ・リンナマーァ) is a witch of the [[Music Squadron|League of Nations Air Forces Aviation Magic Band Luminous Witches]], originating from [[Suomus]]. Her familiar's name is Finn.

==Background==
The sole Ace Witch in the [[Music Squadron|group]]. She was injured in combat which impaired her ability to fight. She took up performing songs at London's [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Albert_Hall Royal Albert Hall]. 

==Personality==
Despite her cool appearance, she is a very impassioned older sister that everyone admires. She is close friends with [[Eleonore Giovanna Gassion|Ellie]].

==Abilities==
Within the unit, she is the main vocalist.

==Equipment==
The Music Squadron uses the [[wikipedia:North Amercian T-6 Texan variants#AT Series (Texan)|AT-6 Texan]] [[Striker Unit|Striker Units]], a trainer made in [[Liberion]]. This Striker is used in the Britannian Commonwealth as well and nicknamed "Harvard" there<ref>[https://twitter.com/humikane/status/1547058338680020992 Humikane's tweet on the AT-6]</ref>.
