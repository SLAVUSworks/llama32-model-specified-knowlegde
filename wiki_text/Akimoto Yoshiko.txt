'''Akimoto Yoshiko''' is [[Miyafuji Yoshika|Yoshika's]] grandmother. She helps her daughter [[Miyafuji Sayaka|Sayaka]] run the [[Miyafuji Clinic]] in [[Fuso]]. It's rumored that her magical powers were once as powerful as Yoshika's when she was young, and that Yoshika has inherited this ability. It is said that when she served in the military, she healed a critically ill patient in an instant, and she once healed about a hundred people in one go.

Yoshiko's inherent magic is [[w:c:powerlisting:Healing|Healing Abilities]] (治癒能力, Chiyu Nōryoku). Classified as a variety of the telekinesis-lineage, it puts a living thing back to how it was by means of magic and is effective not only against external wounds but also diseases.
