'''Alessia Corti '''is a Doctor assigned to the 501st at Pescara base, and tasked with overlooking the health and medical well-being of the witches. Her first appearance was during the Second Season when Miyafuji Yoshika and she examined Miyafuji when she complained of changes in her magic, but she couldn't find anything wrong. Alessia is aged at around her late-twenties and she is stated as being single.Her her mole is also her charm point.

While she is doctor on base she is apparently exclusively tasked with the medical needs of witches specifically and is categorized as a medical specialist for witches and witch medical health. The evident reason for her being tasked to tend only to the witches and in turn witches being tended medically by her: is that a male doctor would be inappropriate. Another factor is that if Alessia were tasked to treat standard soldiers, it is noted they would be lining up with faked illnesses to see her.
