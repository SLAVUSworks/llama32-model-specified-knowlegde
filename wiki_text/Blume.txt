'''W. Blume''' is a witch originating from [[Karlsland]].

==Background==
Blume is an ace from the first [[Neuroi]] war.

She studied aeronautics after her retirement and joined [[Wikipedia:Arado Flugzeugwerke|Arodo Aircraft Manufacturing]] as a technician. After taking over from Dr. Retel, who had transferred to the Bavarian Sky Factory, she continued designing the training aircraft [[Wikipedia:Arado Ar 66|Ar66]] and flew safely for the first time in 32 years. Due to its good results, it was decided to adopt it in large numbers as a basic training aircraft for the Karlsland Air Force.

==Trivia==
*Her ace archetype is [[Wikipedia:Walter Blume (aircraft designer)|Walter Blume]], a WW1 German ace that became a designer after the war.
