At the Allied Forces Northern Headquarters in Helsinki, [[Suomus]], [[Gundula Rall|Gundula]] and [[Edytha Rossmann|Edytha]] give their report to Marshall Mannstein and Mannerheim. Mannstein informs the two that a new Allied army group will be organized in St. Petersburg to counter the [[Neuroi]] hive Grigori. The Marshals request the support of the [[502nd Joint Fighter Wing]] as aerial support for the new army group. When Manstein brings up the question of who the [[Fuso]] witch that is joining the 502nd, Gundula reports the witch is [[Karibuchi Takami|Takami]].

On the plane ride back to base, Gundula explains to Edyatha that reveling that Takami had been sent home could jeopardize supplies from Fuso. Later, Gundula, Edytha, and Sasha try to formulate how the 501st managed to destroy a Neuroi hive. 

[[Karibuchi Hikari|Hikari]] is out training and upon seeing that Edytha can perform the skill of walking across water with her magic, requests that the [[Karlsland]] witch train her. Edytha says Hikari needs to pass a test first and if she cannot accomplish this within a week, Hikari will be send home. [[Nikka Edvardine Katajainen|Nipa]] overhears this and goes to [[Kanno Naoe|Nanoe]] to discuss this.

Hikari's poor training performance illustrates her lack of magic power while both Nipa and Nanoe observe from afar. Edytha tells Hikari to fetch a hat from the top of a tall spire by climbing up it by properly controlling her magic to allow her to climb up the vertical surface. Privately, Edytha confides to Gundula that Hikari is probalby not going to pass the test and it is to show her the limits of her powers.

On the second day, Nipa and Nanoe come to visit Hikari who continues to try to climb the spire after she falls due to a bird who had a nest on the tower. After another day of trying without success, Nipa takes Hikari the sauna to rest. The next two days pass with the Nanoe, Edytha, and Gundula watching as Hikari tries new methods such as not wearing her shoes and not eating as much. On the sixth day, Hikari passes out while climbing from exhaustion and Naoe catches her. Nanoe asks Edytha about the purpose of the test and the Karlsland witch explains that if she cannot climb the spire, then she would likely die in combat if she saw action. 

Hikari wakes up later that night and plans to continue to try to climb the spire. Edytha pulls Hikari alongside to ask what kind of witch she wants to be. Hikari responds that she wants to be like her sister to which Edytha explains that Hikari reminded her of a girl she trained that had a similar demeanor and lacked magical ability. The girl had convinced Edytha to see combat even though the older witch knew she was not ready for such an endeavor. This mistake resulted in the girl never being able to fly again. Hikari understands and reiterates her desire to help the 502nd however she can. 

On the seventh day, heavy winds cause Hikari to lose her balance and fall though she manages to catch herself with her feet to avoid falling on the bird's nest. Edytha advises Hikari to concentrate all her magic into her hands and switch her magic powers to each hand as she climbs up the spire. With Edytha's advice, Hikari continues to climb the spire when the Suoyarvi base reports a medium-sized Neuroi coming from the east. Meanwhile, Hikari is about to give up when Nanoe decides to comes back and uses insults to help give Hikari the motivation to climb up to the top of the spire. Edytha allows Hikari to sortie as promised and Hikari's [[Striker Unit]] is responding better than before. Edytha tells Hikari to be herself and the advices pays off as Hikari performs well against the Neuroi, being able to dodge it's attack. Hikari accidently bumps into the Neuroi and is somehow able to locate the core which allows the rest of the 502nd to know where to concentrate their fire. 

Edytha surmises that Hikari can locate the Neuroi's core by coming into contact with the said Neuroi. This means Hikari has Contact Magic Eye but Gundula forbids Hikari from using it. Gundula and Edytha explain how Takami used her Absolute Magic Eye and it caused her to be severely wounded. They don't want to put Hikari in such danger and have Takami's sacrifice in vain.

== References==
=== Erich von Manstein ===
Born from a Prussian family and seeing action in World War I, [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erich_von_Manstein Manstein] helped rebuild the German military in the inter-war period and during World War II, would initially assist in putting together plans for OKH. Manstein would become famous for his ''Sichelschnitt'' or "sickle cut" which he developed with Heinz Guderian which called for a lightning strike through the weakly defended Ardennes Forest to cut off the British and French troops in Belgium. During Operation Barbarossa, Manstein would command forces at various battles such as at the siege of Sevastopol and Operation Winter Storm which was a failed attempted to relieve the 6th Army at Stalingrad. At the Third Battle of Kharkov, Manstein commanded the German forces and destroyed three Soviet armies in the process. The last major action Manstein would command would be at the Battle of Kursk where he led the southern forces. In March 1944, Manstein was dismissed due to his disagreements on the conduct of war. Manstein would be  captured by the British in August 1945.

=== Carl Gustaf Emil Mannerheim ===
The Finnish commander-in-chief during the Winter War, [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Gustaf_Emil_Mannerheim Mannerheim] would be known for organizing the defense of his country against the Soviet Union. The Mannerheim Line was named after the famous general and held off the Soviets initially even though the defensive line was far from complete and was a flexible defense. A man looking out for Finnish sovereignty, Mannerheim would work hard when negiotiating with both Nazi Germany as well as late the Soviet Union in World War II. As a result of this, Finland would retain it's independence. In 1944, Mannerheim would become the president of Finland and lead them through maintaining the armistice terms with the Soviets. Mannerheim held this position until 1946 when he resigned. 

=== Ju-52 ===
Built by Junkers, this transport aircraft began life as an airline before the war. The Luftwaffe would use the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Junkers_Ju_52 Ju-52] mainly as a transport aircraft as well briefly being a bomber. The ''Tante Ju'' "Aunt Ju" began military service during the Spanish Civil War and into the Second World War, delivering supplies and dropping ''Fallschirmjäger'' into action. The Ju-52 would famous participate in the Battle for The Hague and the Battle of Crete where the transport aircraft deployed ''Fallschirmjäger'' successfully. As the transport aircraft was only capable of 265&nbsp;km/h, it was extremely vulnerable to enemy fighters and anti-aircraft fire which was demonstrated when Ju-52s attempted to deliver supplies to the encircled 6th Army at Stalingrad where 268 Ju-52s were lost during the entire battle. Another example of the vulnerability of the Ju-52 is the Palm Sunday Massacre in North Africa where 24 Ju-52s were shot down by Allied P-40s and Spitfires. Despite this, the Ju-52 would continue to see service until the end of the war and even post-war in Germany, France, and Spain. A total of 4,845 of all variants were produced.
