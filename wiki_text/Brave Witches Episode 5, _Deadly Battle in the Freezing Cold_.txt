==References==
===Kasha===
An East-Central and Eastern European porridge dish made with buckwheat or other grains, it is often baked or boiled with water or milk. [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kasha Kasha] has been a part of Slavic diets for at least a 1,000 years. In Russia, kasha is a national dish and is often consumed with butter. In fact, there is a saying in Russia that states, "щи да каша – пища наша" (shchi da kasha – pishcha nasha) which translates to "Shchi and kasha are our food."

===Shchi===
A traditional Russian soup, [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shchi shchi] is made up of mainly cabbage with meat, mushrooms, flour, and spices as options. Becoming a staple food in Russia due to being easy to cok, it makes up the other part of the Russian saying "щи да каша – пища наша" (shchi da kasha – pishcha nasha) which translates to "Shchi and kasha are our food." Shchi is often consumed with rye bread.
