'''Brave Witches Prequel: The Vast Land of Orussia''' (ブレイブウィッチーズPrequel オラーシャの大地, ''Burēbu Witchīzu Prequel: Orussia no Daichi'') is a manga series that was released in Newtype magazine from April 2017 to October 2017. It is also available in Kindle digital books. This the manga version of [[ Brave Witches Prequel]] Volume 1 light novel.

A story depicting the days before the anime "Brave Witches". Forefront of defending humanity from the invasion of Neuroi, the [[502nd Joint Fighter Wing]] is created in Petersburg. The story follows the new members of the 502nd as they join the team. [[Kanno Naoe]], who wants to call [[Karibuchi Takami]], an ace of the Fuso Empire, deepens ties with her new friends while struggling to increase her number of shots down.
