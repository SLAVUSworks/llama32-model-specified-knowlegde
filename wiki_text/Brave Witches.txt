'''''Brave Witches''''' (ブレイブウィッチーズ, ''Bureibu Witchīzu'') is a spin-off anime of the main ''Strike Witches'' anime series. Originally created by Humikane Shimada via a series of magazine illustration columns. The anime features the [[502nd Joint Fighter Wing]].

Set immediately after the first season of [[Strike Witches]]. The [[Gallia|Gallian]] Hive had just been destroyed, and a new Hive threatens the 502nd. The story focuses on the adventures of [[Karibuchi Hikari]] and her enlistment to the 502nd Joint Fighter Wing stationed at Petersburg, [[Orussia]].

[[Strike Witches 2]] takes place after the events of Brave Witches.

==Production Delays==
As announced by "[http://www.animenewsnetwork.com/daily-briefs/2016-10-23/brave-witches-anime-4th-episode-delayed-by-1-week/.108004 Anime News Network]", the official website of the Brave Witches spinoff television [http://w-witch.jp/onair/ anime announced] last Thursday that the airing of the anime's fourth episode has been delayed by a week due to delays in the production schedule. All networks airing the anime will instead air the Strike Witches: Operation Victory Arrow "St. Trond's Thunder" episode.
