'''Brave Witches: 502nd Joint Fighter Wing''' (ブレイブウィッチーズ 第502統合戦闘航空団, ''Burēbu Witchīzu: Dai 502 Tōgō Sentō Kōkūdan'') is a manga series that was published in the Monthly Shonen Ace magazine. It is also available as a Kindle eBook.

This is the manga adaptation of the [[Brave Witches|Brave Witches anime]]. It depicts all major events taking place in the anime.

Thanks to the work of the 501st Joint Fighter Wing "Strike Witches", the Republic of [[Gallia]] has been liberated, but the threat of the Neuroi has not yet left the world. The stage of the upcoming battle is the base of the Orussian Empire, St. Petersburg. The magical girls (witches) of the 502nd Joint Fighter Wing "Brave Witches" fly around the cold skies of Eastern Europe!
