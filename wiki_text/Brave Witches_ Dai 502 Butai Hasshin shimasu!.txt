'''Brave Witches: 502nd Joint Fighter Wing Take Off!''' (ブレイブウィッチーズ 第502部隊発進しますっ！, ''Burēbu Witchīzu: Dai 502 Butai Hasshin shimasu!'') is a manga series illustrated by Fujibayashi Makoto. It was published in Young Ace Up magazine from October 2016 to June 2017. It is also available in Kindle digital book. It contains both 4-koma and classic manga formats.

This manga is a humoristic take on the series and features the [[502nd Joint Fighter Wing]] during their everyday lives.
