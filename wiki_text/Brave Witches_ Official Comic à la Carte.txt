'''Brave Witches: Official Comic à la Carte''' (ブレイブウィッチーズ 公式コミックアラカルト, ''Burēbu Witchīzu kōshiki komikku arakaruto'') is a manga compilation of short stories by various artists.

Brave Witches, finally leading the European counterattack! In addition to the Karibuchi sisters, this manga features a unique anthology that allows the reader to enjoy unique girls such as Nipa, Kanno, Krupinski, etc.!
