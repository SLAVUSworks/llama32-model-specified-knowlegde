The '''Commonwealth of Britannia''' (ブリタニア連邦 <sup>[[Wikipedia:Help:Installing Japanese character sets|?]]</sup>) is a country in [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_Europe northwestern Europe], which is similar to modern [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom Great Britain]. It is the setting for most of the [[Strike Witches|first season of Strike Witches]].

Their nation's insignia seems to be a small red disk on a larger blue circle superimposed on a wider white circle outlined with another yellow circle. Britannia was the Front Line of the Neuroi War and the Battle for Gallia.

Prominent cities of note listed on the world Atlas are [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turnhouse Turnhouse], [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow Glasgow], [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cranwell Cranwell], [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham Birmingham], [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London London], and [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plymouth Plymouth].

Britannia is a maritime nation that has monopolized trade from [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlantic_Ocean the Atlantic Ocean] to [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Ocean the Indian Ocean]. Thanks to the efforts of [[501st Joint Fighter Wing|the 501st JFW]] in defeating the Gallian hive by the end of 1944: Britannia is a peaceful land without the threat of Neuroi invasion hanging over it any longer.
