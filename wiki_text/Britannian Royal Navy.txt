The Britannian Royal Navy is the naval military branch for the [[Britannia|Commonwealth of Britannia]].

==History==
The Britannian Royal Navy was first seen offshore of Europe in 1943 with three [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_George_V-class_battleship_(1939) ''King George V''-class battleships] being part of the Allied fleet bombarding the Neuroi. However, a large flying Neuroi attacks the fleet and at least one of the Britannian battleships is damaged by a Neuroi beam that struck it. The carrier ''[https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Victorious_(R38) Victorious]'' was dispatched to be one of the three carriers to form the [[508th Joint Fighter Wing]] in the Atlantic. 

Later in 1945, the Britannian navy provides escort for an important convoy bound for Murmansk that contains equipment that is important for the upcoming operation to defeat the Grigori hive. The convoy came under attack and when [[Nora Taylor]], the Fleet Air Arm witch assigned to the convoy is shot down, it falls to the Britannian navy to protect the Liberty ship ''Ellsworth'' which is carrying important equipment. The destroyer ''Middleton'' was severely damaged in the process by the Neuroi attack and the light cruiser ''Odysseus'' moves ahead to use itself as a shield to protect the convoy. The light cruiser is about to be hit by Neuroi beams before four witches of the 502nd intervene and turn the tide of the battle. With their combined efforts of the convoy ships and the witches, they are able to defeat the Neuroi and protect the convoy. 

In the Adriatic that same year, the battleship ''Prince of Wales'' was one of the three major warships that provided escort for the assault on Malta. During Operation Mars, the Britannia navy dispatches the battleships ''Prince of Wales'', ''Duke of York'', ''Anson'', and ''Howe'' to protect the right flank of the Allied fleet. The four Britannian battleships fired broadsides to destroy incoming Neuroi to allow the ''Yamato'' the opportunity to go after the Neuroi hive. After the ''Yamato''<nowiki/>'s attack was unsuccessful, the Britannian battleships attempted to destroy the core with their main battery but the Neuroi used Mio's shield to block the shells before they could do damage. After the [[501st Joint Fighter Wing]] managed to destroy the hive, the Britannian battleships sailed with the rest of the Allied fleet back to base.

==Trivia==

* The Britannian Royal Navy is based on the real life British [[Wikipedia:Royal_Navy|Royal Navy]].
* While there were no ''Bellona''-class cruisers named "Odysseus" in reality, given that this is the Greek form of "Ulysses" and that she was seen escorting Arctic convoys, this most likely makes her an intentional reference to the Alistair MacLean novel [[Wikipedia:HMS_Ulysses_(novel)|HMS Ulysses]].
* The three Britannian ships seen in The Movie reuse the ''King George V''-class 3D models, which presents a continuity issue as all (or nearly all) of that fleet was sunk; this also applies to the Karlsland, Venezian, Romagnan, and Fuso ships present. The simplest explanation would be that this short scene is simply a case of model reuse, and canonically the ships could be considered to be different, similar classes of ship.
