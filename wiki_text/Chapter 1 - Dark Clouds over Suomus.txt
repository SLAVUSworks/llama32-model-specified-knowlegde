<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“T-This is bad! Really bad!”</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; "><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: sans-serif; font-style: normal; line-height: 19px; ">Häkkinen</span>’s voice returned to its usual sobriety as she tended to the wounded.</span>

''<span lang="EN-US" style="font-family:Tahoma;mso-fareast-language:JA">The army was equipped with ordinary weaponry, and could provide no opposition to a bombardment.</span>''<span lang="EN-US" style="font-family: Tahoma; "> </span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">From outside the window, F/L </span><span style="color: rgb(0, 0, 0); font-family: sans-serif; font-style: normal; line-height: 19px; ">Häkkinen</span><span style="font-family: Tahoma; "> observed the widespread damage done to the base.</span>
