<p sab="471">“Ooy! Tomoko! Yesterday was so hectic wasn't it?”



<p sab="472">The person calling out like that as she entered the dining hall with a huge smile on her face, was Pilot Officer Katherine O'Hare. Walking beside her; silently absorbed in a book, was the figure of Flight Sergeant Ursula Hartmann.



<p sab="473">They were stationed at Kauhava Airbase in Suomus. Just last week, Tomoko and the members of the Independent Volunteer Air squadron, along with Flight Lieutenant Ahonen's 1<sup sab="474">st</sup> Squadron, and Flight Lieutenant Rudel's dive-bombing squadron, had collaborated to fight back the Neuroi force advancing across the national border. Thus re-capturing this airbase.



<p sab="475">Situated near the town of Slussen, the base had now become an important spot for intercepting the Neuroi. Since recovering both Slussen and Kauhava Airbase, the Neuroi's offensives had weakened in intensity.



<p sab="476">There was still an air-raid practically every day, but the number of invading aircraft did not exceed half of what they were last month.



<p sab="477">Accordingly, the squadrons stationed at Kauhava were steadily increasing their military achievements, but...



<p sab="478">“What's wrong Tomoko?”



<p sab="479">Katherine stared into Tomoko's face with a concerned expression. It wouldn't take a well endowed, blond Liberion girl to point out that Tomoko's face was lacking in radiance.



<p sab="480">From behind her sleek black hair, cut off neatly at different levels; her beautiful narrow eyes just gazed out blankly. A beautiful girl by any standards, but her perfectly proportioned face; every part that of a Fusan doll, was motionless as if frozen.



<p sab="481">Her Khaki coloured military clothes were missing a button from one sleeve, and having not been brushed off in a while; looked quiet tatty. For Tomoko; who usually maintained a sharp image, this was a rare lapse in her appearance.



<p sab="482">It was like she had suddenly aged inside.



<p sab="483">“You shot down seven aircraft by yourself yesterday, aren't you even a little bit pleased~?”



<p sab="484">Katherine clapped Tomoko on the shoulder. With that, Tomoko finally raised her face as if she had only just noticed Katherine was there.

<p sab="493">At first she opposed the Ki-44, and seemed to despise her new assistant, but due to her inexperience with love she was eventually entranced by him.



<p sab="494">Consequently, Tomoko and Itokawa became quite close... the goddess of fate however, was extremely cruel to them on this occasion.
