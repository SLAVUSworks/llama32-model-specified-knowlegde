<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“Well… given our current situation you can’t really do anything about it.” Elma was next.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“Fine, fine! I’ll do it! I’ll sleep with you!” Tomoko yelled.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“Really!?”</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“I wouldn’t go back on my word to a girl.”</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">As she heard those words, Haruka reached into her pocket with a deep breath and pulled out a pair of gigantic glasses.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">They were a pair of perfectly thick glasses.</span>

''<span lang="EN-US" style="font-family:Tahoma;mso-fareast-language:JA">She’s going to put those on? Goodness gracious, they really are huge… They’re so huge you could probably wear them underwater.</span>''

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">Tomoko snickered in a lowered voice.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“… Did you just laugh?”</span><span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">Everyone collectively drew closer to Tomoko.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“What! O-of course I-I didn’t laugh! Well then, let’s get moving!”</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">The five of them once again began their assault. </span><span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">It was the same plan as before – they would form behind Katharine and use her as a shield for Haruka's weapon.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">The Diomedia drew closer.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">400 metres, 300 metres... when it reached 200 metres, Haruka raised her head and placed her 20mm motor cannon on Katharine's shoulder.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">The thick cartridge cases flipped out with a Don! Don! Don! The cannons had been sucked into the turret. The foremost shell entered into the turret, and then exploded.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“Good job!”</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">Haruka lined up her next shot, and that one hit too! She continued to shoot, and took down four of the turrets. There was now space to get above the Diomedia.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">The distance was now 500 metres.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">Haruka and Katharine withdrew.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“Ursula!” Tomoko shouted.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">Ursula let out a sigh before launching the rocket she had been holding in both hands. The rocket launched with a dry ‘Schpon!’ noise. </span><span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">The rocket entered the Diomedia's body with a plume of white smoke. </span><span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">It exploded violently with a Boom! Leaving a clear hole dead in the centre of the Diomedia.[[File:Th724_d_217.jpg|thumb|The rocket launched with a dry 'Schpon!' Noise]]</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">However... the Diomedia was indeed very hardy, and continued to fly.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“P/O Elma! Support me, full speed ahead!”</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“Roger that!”</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">Tomoko grabbed onto Elma from behind, and used her to increase her acceleration.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“Uuuuoooooooooooooooooooooo!”</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">Tomoko flew towards the hole where the rocket had burst the Diomedia. She straightened her body up and made the plunge, her katana gripped tightly in both hands.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“P/O Elma, pull off!”</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">Elma separated herself from Tomoko. With her katana ready, Tomoko flew into the open hole on the Diomedia. In an instant, her figure had disappeared inside the Diomedia...</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">The bulk of the Diomedia broke off. It burst into flames as bright as strobe lighting. In an instant, the piece that had been broken off heaved, before exploding completely.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">The inside of the body was wreathed in flames from the explosion. Afterwards... the space that the Diomedia had been in was empty. </span><span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">Debris flew haphazardly at Haruka and the others, who hadn't been near the explosion.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“Tomoko!”</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“Pilot Officer Anabuki!” Haruka and the others screamed.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">The Diomedia had been split into four pieces – all leaving behind a trail of white smoke...</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">For a second, there was dead silence...</span><span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">Even though it was only for a second, it felt like it had lasted for all eternity.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">What had happened to Tomoko?</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">Everyone stood stunned, staring longingly at the cloud of white smoke.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“No way… and just as P/O Anabuki had promised that she’d sleep with me…”</span><span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">In the instant that Haruka spoke... a white figure shone from within the smoke.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“I’m alright.” They heard Tomoko's voice over the wireless.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">During the explosion... Tomoko had protected herself with her magic field. The remaining misfits gave an exuberant cheer.</span>





<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">Tomoko headed towards where her friends were so that they could return to base in formation.</span>

<span style="font-family: Tahoma; ">“Pilot Officer Beurling… we took down our true enemy.” Tomoko whispered as she stared absent mindedly at the trail of smoke.</span>
