'''Christiane Barkhorn''' (クリスティアーネ・バルクホルン<sup>[http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Installing_Japanese_character_sets ?]</sup>)

[[Gertrud Barkhorn|Trude]]'s younger sister. Chris was seriously injured prior to the events of season 1, leaving her in a coma. She eventually comes to, and recovers somewhat. Trude cares about her sister deeply, as evidenced by her actions in the second season where she has [[Miyafuji Yoshika]], whom she bears a resemblance to, buy a dress for Chris, and then she tries to get [[Hanna-Justina Marseille|Marseille's]] autograph for her as Chris is a fan of Marseille.
