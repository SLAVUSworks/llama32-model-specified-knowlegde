'''Chris Keera''' is a witch originating from [[Gallia]] and affiliated with the Gallia Intelligence Agency.

==Background==
Right after the mysterious bombing of the 506 A-team’s Dijon base, Chris arrives on the scene, claiming to work for the Gallia Intelligence Agency.

However, this claim was a farce, rather Chris works for an organization that was partnered with a radical faction in the Gallia Intelligence Agency. The name “Chris Keera” is a pseudonym she used from a stolen identity.

==Personality==
Chris is a cold, ruthless character who will stop at no end to achieve her goals and complete her mission.

==Abilities==
Chris is skilled in physical combat and utilizes a variety of tools at her disposal, such as cord she used to nearly strangle [[Marian Carl]] during their first confrontation in the skies. However as a witch, her magical power is declining due to age, so she cannot raise an effective magical shield any more.
