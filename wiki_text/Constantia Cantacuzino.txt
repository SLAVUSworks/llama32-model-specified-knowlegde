'''Constantia Cantacuzino''' (コンスタンティア・カンタクジノ) is a [[Night Witch]] of the [[505th Joint Fighter Wing]], originating from [[Dacia]]. She is occasionally referred to as "Miss Impaler" and "Noble Vampire" by [[Grete Gollob]].

==Background==
Constantia was born into a prosperous duke family, and from childhood she had excelled in all sorts of sporting activities. She entered an aviation school owned by one of her relatives and received flying training. She finished basic flight training in just 2 weeks. She received the advanced flying license and purchased an aircraft and an old Striker Unit for private use so that she could polish her aviation acrobatics. She was so skilled that she became Dacia's champion in aviation acrobatics in 1939, just before the [[Neuroi]] invasion.

She has the ability to use any kind of Striker Units. She would get her hands on [[Orussia]]n and [[Liberion]] units on the battlefield and use them without any problems right from the first flight. She is also able to pilot large transport aircraft. Her rank was Reserve Flying Officer and when the Neuroi invasion began, she was called to action. She was assigned to the 53rd Flying Corps which had Dacia's most talented witches.

Only a week after she started her assignment she got her first victory. Her kill count kept growing and she got promoted to Reserve Flight Lieutenant, and because of her family's influence, she was taken out from the front lines. When the Neuroi offensive intensified, even the upper stratum didn't wish to keep her down when she requested to return to the front lines. She was assigned to the 7th Fighter Group after this.

When Dacia fell, she fought to the bitter end to make sure people were able to take refuge in Bucharest. It was said that she had achieved quite the amount of military accomplishments, but since there were no real witnesses, and her remaining silent about her less important kills, no records were kept.

After Dacia fell she took part in battle again when she joined with the strike force led by [[Grete Gollob|Gollob]] in Altland. At the end of "the longest evacuation war" she was recruited into the [[505th Joint Fighter Wing]]. When the [[506th Joint Fighter Wing|506th]] was formed it was said that the Allied headquarters wanted her in it, but the [[Fuso]] army was against it.

==Abilities==
Her inherent magic is to manipulate bullets that she already fired. She can also re-accelerate them, even if they are stopped. This is why she calls her magic ability "Bullet re-accelerator".

She also possessed a degree of [[w:c:powerlisting:Night Vision|night vision]], but not on a viable level, so the night missions that were lit only by a handful of barrels proved to be dangerous.
