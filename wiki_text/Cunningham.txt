'''Cunningham''' is a night witch originating from [[Britannia]]. She is commonly known as "Cat's Eyes".

==Background==
Originally fighting as a night witch, Cunningham later became a test pilot for [[Wikipedia:De_Havilland|De Havillander]].

==Trivia==
Her ace archetype is [[Wikipedia:John_Cunningham_(RAF_officer)|John "Cat's Eyes" Cunningham]].
