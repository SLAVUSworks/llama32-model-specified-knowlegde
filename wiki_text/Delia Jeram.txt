'''Delia M. Jeram''' (デリア・M・ジェラム) is a Witch of the [[508th Joint Fighter Wing]], originating from [[Britannia]].

==Background==
Delia has soft, ladylike manners, but she is sometimes polite only superficially. Even though she is personality-wise the complete opposite of Flying Officer [[Jamie Swett|Swett]], they get along surprisingly well. Because they work well together their military gains are numerous.

When the [[508th Joint Fighter Wing|508th JFW]] was established she was sent to it with the carrier [[Wikipedia:HMS Victorious (R38)|Victorious]].

==Trivia==
*Her ace archetype is [http://www.cieldegloire.fr/002_raf_jeram_d_m.php Dennis Jeram]. Jeram was seconded/loaned to the RAF during the Battle of Britain where he flew Hurricanes. Mk1 & Mk2 Hurricanes were converted into Sea Hurricanes for the RN to provide fighter protection for the fleet. On return to the Royal Navy, Jeram was posted to 888 RNS stationed on HMS Formidable later equipped with F4F Wildcats, called Martlets by the RN Fleet Air Arm.
