'''Dolores Bader''' (ドロレス・バーダー) is a witch originating from [[Britannia]].

==Background==
Dolores is the Squadron Leader of the [[Her Majesty's Witch|11th Joint Fighter Squadron: Her Majesty's Witch]], in charge of air defense within and around Britannia.

The HMW squadron is composed mostly of Britannia Army witches, but also exiled witches from Britannia's Federation nations such as [[Faraway Land]], and eastern countries such as [[Gallia]], [[Ostmark]], [[Dacia]], and [[Moesia]], enlarging the Fighter Wing. Some peoples within the Air Force argued that it should be a Joint Fighter Wing, but they were treated as a standard combat squadron because they disliked being used for missions other than defense.
