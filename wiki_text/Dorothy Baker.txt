'''Dorothy Baker''' (ドロシー・ベイカー) is a Witch of the [[508th Joint Fighter Wing]], originating from [[Liberion]].

==Background==
She was on aircraft carrier [[Wikipedia:USS_Enterprise_(CV-6)|Enterprise]]. She was selected to be an additional member of the [[508th Joint Fighter Wing|508th JFW]] when Enterprise was dispatched. She was injured at the time.

==Trivia==
*Her ace archetype is [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Baker_(aviator) Douglas Baker].
