'''Edith M. la Meslée''', full name "Edith Marie la Meslée", is a witch originating from [[Gallia]].

==Background==
One of the few Gallian witches who played an active role in the withdrawal battle of Gallia, and the top ace of said withdrawal battle. She withdrew to Africa where her military gains increased, before serving in a coastal patrol unit in [[Britannia]].
