'''Edytha Prinzessin zur Lippe-Weissenfeld''' is a Night Witch originating from [[Karlsland]] and affiliated with the Karlsland Air Force.

==Background==
Edytha comes from a royal family which have a vast territory in Karlsland and Ostmark, the birthplace of a famous aristocrat who has connections to various parts of Europe. She has the title of a Princess (Prinzessin).

At the time of establishment of the [[506th Joint Fighter Wing]], Edytha was selected as a possible candidate for Fighter Captain on the recommendation of Group Captain [[Heinrike von Bülow-Bothkamp]], ​​along with Flight Lieutenant [[Heinrike Prinzessin zu Sayn-Wittgenstein]].

She has high fighting capabilities, and she's also good at commanding.

==Trivia==
*Her ace archetype is [[Wikipedia:Egmont Prinz zur Lippe-Weißenfeld|Egmont Prinz zur Lippe-Weißenfeld]], a night fighter ace of royal descent.
