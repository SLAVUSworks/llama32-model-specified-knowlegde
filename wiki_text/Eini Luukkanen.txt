'''Eini Antia Luukkanen''' (エイニ・アンティア・ルーッカネン) is a witch originating from [[Suomus]]. Her nickname is "Invincible Luukkanen".

==Background==
Eini is the commanding officer of Suomus Air Force 24th Squadron.

When she received a Bf109 [[Striker Unit]], her old F2A Buffalo No. BW-393 was transferred 
to [[Hanna Wind]].
