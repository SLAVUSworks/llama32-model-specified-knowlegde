'''Elma Leivonen''' (エルマ・レイヴォネン) is a witch of the [[507th Joint Fighter Wing]], originating from [[Suomus]].

==Background==
A Flying Officer from the Suomus Air Force, Elma was the previous squadron commander of the [[507th Joint Fighter Wing|Suomus Independent Volunteer Air Squadron]], but handed her position over to Tomoko after the fall of Slussen.

Even though she lacks self-confidence and is therefore constantly apologising, there is no doubt about her natural aerial ability.

She is on junior/senior terms with [[Eila Ilmatar Juutilainen]].

==Personality==
Elma is a timid and clumsy girl who is constantly falling over, but she tries her best to be supporting and positive.

==Trivia==

* Her familiar is a [[Wikipedia:Mink|mink]].
* Elma has no known archetype. [[Wikipedia:Noboru_Yamaguchi_(author)|Yamaguchi Noboru]], the author of the Suomus Misfits novels, said himself that Elma was not based on an existing archetype.<ref>[https://twitter.com/hexagonzero/status/163644189425401856 Tweet by Yamaguchi Noboru (Elma's archetype)] (in Japanese)</ref> She was speculated to be based on the third highest-scoring Finnish ace [[Wikipedia:Eino_Luukkanen|Eino Luukkanen]] before a witch named [[Eini Luukkanen]] was introduced in the Witches of Aurora manga.
* According to [[Mika Ahonen]], Elma's callsign was "Skylark"(''Hibari'' /ひばり) prior to becoming a member of the Suomus Air Force Independent Volunteer Squadron.
