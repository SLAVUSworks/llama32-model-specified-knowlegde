Emiliana Lucchini is [[Francesca Lucchini]]'s Mother.

==Background==
Her husband is a ship's captain in foreign seas, and is often away for long periods of time. Partly because of that, and with the [[Neuroi]] invasion around [[Venezia]], she evacuated from Roma to her parent's home in Sicilia.

She is good at cooking, especially seafood dishes, which are Lucchini's favorite.
