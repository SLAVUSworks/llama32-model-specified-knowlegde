'''Emma Luening''' is a Witch of the [[505th Joint Fighter Wing]].

==Background==
Most information on Emma Luening is completely unknown, other than her depictions in [[World Witches: Contrail of Witches|Contrail of Witches]] as being someone who is seemingly close to [[Grete Gollob]] with an unknown history together.

Sometime after the "Longest Evacuation War" campaign, and after [[Dacia]] fell, Emma fought alongside Grete and the rest of the "Mirage Witches" in Altland. She was also present for when [[Inufusa Yuno]] and [[Constantia Cantacuzino]] were first recruited into the 505th JFW.

==Appearances==
'''Manga'''  
*[[World Witches: Contrail of Witches]]| Vol.2

== Trivia== 
*She is on a first-name basis with Grete, which Inufusa eventually points out despite Grete's desire to drop the subject.
*Her magical familiar seems to be a feline of some sort.
*Emma Luening is based off Gordon Gollob's wife, Elisabeth Lüning.
