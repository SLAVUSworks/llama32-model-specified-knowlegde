'''Enrica Tarantola''' is a Witch originating from [[Romagna]]. She is nicknamed "Banana".

==Background==
Her parents' house is a greengrocer store.

She is a member of the Red Pants and served as the wingman of Squadron Leader [[Federica N. Doglio|Doglio]] for a time. During the defense battle of Malta, she met with Flying Officer [[Patricia Schade|Schade]] of Britannia, who was later invited to join the 504th JFW.
