'''Franziska Götz''' (フランツィスカ・ゴッツ<sup>[http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Installing_Japanese_character_sets ?]</sup>) is a Witch from [[Karlsland]] and attached to the Karlsland Air Force.

==Background==
The commander of the [[Wikipedia:Jagdgeschwader_26|Jagdgeschwader 26 (JG26)]], she is adored by her subordinates and affectionately called "Mother" (お母さん, ''okaasan'') by them. Having enlisted as a Witch since before the war, she was originally assigned to the [[Wikipedia:Jagdgeschwader_53|Jagdgeschwader 53 (JG53)]].
