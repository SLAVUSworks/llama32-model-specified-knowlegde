'''Friederica Losigkeit''' (フレデリカ・ロージヒカイト<sup>[http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Installing_Japanese_character_sets ?]</sup>) is a Witch attached with the [[Karlsland|Karlsland Air Force]]. She is the commander of the [[Wikipedia:Jagdgeschwader_1_(World_War_II)#Group_I..2FJG_1|Jagdgeschwader 1 Gruppe I (I./JG 1)]].

==Background==
There is a possibility that she is the same individual as the Friederike Losigkeit (フリーデリケ・ロージヒカイト) that appeared in [[Strike Witches: Sōkū no Otome-tachi|Soukuu no Otome-tachi]].
