'''Friederike Porsche''' (フレデリカ・ポルシェ<sup>[http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Installing_Japanese_character_sets ?]</sup>) is a witch from [[Karlsland]].

==Background==
A Former ground attack strike witch that was fighting in Karlsland at the beginning of the war. She nearly lost all of her magical power after being wounded during the withdrawal battle of Karlsland. Despite this, she was dispatched to North Africa where she commands a Karlsland Division with an experimental Tiger land striker unit attached. Her lover named Hauptmann [[Michael Schmitt]] is a former Panzer commander and serves under her in the same division. He is also her reason why she keeps fighting against the [[Neuroi]].

Despite her age and degrading in magical ability, by the time of 1942 Friederike could still muster enough magical ability to fight if need be: operate MG-34 in akimbo with limited barrier ability ([[Africa Doujinshi#Tiger in Desert 1|Tiger in Desert 1]]); operate a Stuka Strike unit with 37mm cannon ([[Africa Doujinshi#Witches of the Sphinx 3|Witches of the Sphinx 3]]) and again, MG-34 in akimbo ([[Africa_Doujinshi#Witches_of_the_Sphinx 4|The Witches Of The Sphinx 4]]).

Though by regulation romance between a common soldier (no matter he is rank & file / NCO / officer) and a witch is usually not allowed, the story of Friederike and Schmitt's was one that, according to Marseille at the end of [[Africa Doujinshi#Tiger in Desert 2|Tiger in Desert 2]], was pretty well known - and envied - amongst Karlsland witches.
