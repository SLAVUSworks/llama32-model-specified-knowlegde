'''Fukuhara Katsumi''' (福原捷美) is a Witch from [[Fuso]] and attached with the [[Fuso Imperial Army]].

==Background==
During the Fuso sea incident in 1937, she was the recon witch for the second mobile task fleet.

She was the witch that detected Squadron Leader [[Kitagou Fumika]] when she disobeyed orders and attacked the second fleet in order to prevent the ships from launching an unplanned anti-air attack that would have killed the other witches. Katsumi later warned the witches from the incoming attack using the radio, and told them to withdraw from the area.

==Abilities==
Her inherent magic is a type of non-visual perception that can detect objects, sorting out their position, number and classification.
