== Trivia ==

* The Fuso Imperial Navy is based on the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_Japanese_Navy Imperial Japanese Navy].
* While not built in our reality, the four ''Kii''-class battleships feature prominently in the [[Strike Witches Zero:1937 Fuso Sea Incident]] manga, which includes a very lengthy semi-real, semi-fictional overview of technical specifications and history of the ships.
* The ''Kii''-class section also explains that all four ''Amagi''-class battlecruisers were converted to aircraft carriers (as opposed to only ''Akagi'' being completed as a carrier in reality) which explains the existence of ''Amagi'' as a carrier sister ship to ''Akagi'' in this universe; the other two were sold to [[Karlsland]]. This section also makes a passing reference to the ''Nagato''-class battleships, confirming that pair's existence in this universe.
* The eight ''Kagerou''-class destroyers seen in the first season of the anime all feature their division number and name painted on the hull, allowing for the identification of each; the first four listed above make up the 16th Destroyer Division, the latter four the 17th Destroyer Division. At least one was sunk during the first battle, while at least two survived.
* One of the ''Kagerous'' makes a cameo appearance at Yokosuka Naval Arsenal in Strike Witches 2. Although her name isn't quite legible, the "16" is, and the name is four characters long, making her ''Yukikaze'' or ''Hatsukaze''.
* The six ''Akizuki''-class destroyers featured in the second season and movie also feature their names written on the hull, however only the three named above are seen close enough as to be legible. Of the other three, one is presumably ''Teruzuki'', the fourth member of the 61st Destroyer Division alongside the three already confirmed by name.
* As the two named ''Yuugumo''-class destroyers belonged to the 31st Destroyer Division, the two unnamed are presumably ''Asashimo'' and ''Naganami'', that quartet forming the final iteration of the 31st.
