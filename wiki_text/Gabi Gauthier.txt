'''Gabi Gauthier''' is a witch originating from [[Gallia]].

==Background==
After the fall of Gallia, she was dispatched to defend north Africa. She fought together with the famous witch of Africa, Flying Officer [[Hanna-Justina Marseille|Marseille]].
