'''[http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nasser Gaman Abdel Nasser]'''

An Egyptian military officer who will later became the 2nd president of the Republic of Egypt. He is a proud man who cares deeply for his country, and his people. He fights beside his men in the field to encourage them, boosting their morale even in the most desperate of odds stacked against them. The men under his command are a crack, effective fighting force; being able to fight fiercely against [[Neuroi]] invasion even despite their equipment being limited and under-powered against the armor of the Stronger Neuroi units.

What Captain Nasser and his men lack in powerful equipment however; they make up for in fighting spirit, courage, speed and squad teamwork efficacy. A good example of this is when Neuroi assaulted the Al Ashiev Market: Gaman Nasser and his men that were operating an anti-tank gun (possibly a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordnance_QF_2-pounder Ordnance QF 2-pounder]), and were able to fire two rounds at a large, tall, Three legged Neuroi. While the anti-tank guns ordnance was too small and under powered to cause any significant damage to the advancing Neuroi; the crew was able to fire two shots in very quick succession of each other and were able temporarily slow the Neuroi they were attacking by knocking it a bit off balance; all the while Nasser shouted words of encouragement to his men.

He distrusts Witches due to the allied policy to only defend targets of military importance instead of the people of Egypt. He also appears bitter of the allied commands priorities in the deployment of witches and conventional personnel by extension. However, despite this he holds sincere feelings of professional respect towards [[Thomas Edward Lawrence|Lawrence of Arabia]], who has been trying to aid and assist the people of Egypt.

It is thanks to the harrowing acts of not only Ruko but the Witches of the  31st Joint Fighter Squadron Africa, The Patton Girls, Major Miles and 4th Armoured Brigade, C Squadron and Charlotte in her Tiger I and crew at coming to the rescue of Ruko and the Egyption evacuation force in Al Ashiev Market; that Captain Nasser's initial, staunch distrust and strong misgivings towards witches; were replaced with an earnest respect, appreciation and a deep gratitude.
