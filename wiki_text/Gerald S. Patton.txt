'''Gerald S. Patton''' (ジェラルド・S・パットン) was the general placed in command of [[Liberion]] troops in North Africa after the rout and near destruction of the [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1st_Armored_Division_(United_States) 1st Armored Division.]

After getting rid of a number of incompetent commanders, Patton began enforcing ridged discipline, while working to establish a more flexible training system for the employment of land-battle witches and supporting combined arms. Though some considered him to be a cruel martinet, the gratitude of many of the land-battle witches was such that they began to call themselves [[Patton Girls#Known Members|'Patton's Girls.']]

Though there would initially be strained relations between Patton and his Karlsland and Britannian counterparts [[Erwin Rommel|Rommel]] and [[Bernard Law Montgomery|Montgomery,]] respectively, ([[Africa Doujinshi#Tiger in Desert 1|Tiger in the Desert 1 and 2]]) those differences would be resolved, allowing for the vital cooperation needed to conduct Operation Sphinx, ([[Africa Doujinshi|Witches in Africa]]) the first attempt to recapture the [http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suez_Canal Suez Canal.]

Though involved in a life-threatening accident on December 21, 1950, ([[Africa Doujinshi#Witches of the Sphinx 1|Witches of the Sphinx 1]]) similar to the one which killed his real-world counterpart five years earlier; the Liberion Patton survived. ([[Africa Doujinshi#Witches of the Sphinx 5|Witches of the Sphinx 5]])

He is shown to have a liking to [[Charlotte Lueder]] and the [[Patton Girls]], and seems to enjoy shooting at Neuroi with his revolver. He holds the witches in highest regard and is highly protective of them. This can be illustrated clearly where he derided and refused to carry out orders that instructed him leaving Charlotte and her Tiger I to "be abandoned for the bigger picture" to face a Neuroi attack in force alone, and when one of his Patton Girls (likely [[Patricia]] it seems) offered him a light for his cigar; he kindly but firmly declined and told her that such an act is beneath her: that the witches instead serve to warm his heart. And during the meeting of the three generals when Patton argued with Rommel and Montgomery; he referred to the witches protectively as "His Angels".

Patton, alongside Monty; have also apparently read many of Rommel's books on warfare. He was the one who started the plan to liberate Berlin and Karlsland, called the "Operation Southwind".

==Trivia==
* During his rescue of Rommel during Operation Sphinx; Patton makes reference to "Custer's Last Stand" and equating Rommel's desperate situation during the operation as being akin to facing a similar, dire fate as Custer; this reference implies that the The Battle of the Little Bighorn occurred at some point in Liberion's history. This does however raise the question of what the exact details of the event were like given the specific historical and cultural contexts that differ strongly from real world history.
