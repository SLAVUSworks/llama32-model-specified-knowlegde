'''Giovanna Bonet''' is a Witch originating from [[Romagna]].

==Background==
She is part of the [[Wikipedia:it:Squadriglia complementare d'allarme "Montefusco-Bonet"|Bonet Auxiliary Squadron]] that supports the [[Liberion]] 325th Fighter Group in the defense of Romagna. Although the squadron should normally only do support, in reality during their missions they dispatch like the 325th Fighter Squadron and defend together.
