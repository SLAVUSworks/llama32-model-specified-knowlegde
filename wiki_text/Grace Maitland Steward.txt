'''Grace Maitland Steward''' (グレイス・メイトランド・スチュワード) is a former witch and the manager of the [[Music Squadron|League of Nations Air Forces Aviation Magic Band Luminous Witches]], originating from [[Liberion]] and serving in the [[Liberion Army]]. Her nickname is Gracie (グレーシー, ''Gurēshī'').

==Background==
During her active service as a witch, she participated in many battles, including the retreat from Dunkirk and the Battle of Britannia, as a part of the Liberion Volunteer Army.

At the time of the Dunkirk evacuation, Liberion had not yet begun to seriously send its military force (witches) to Europe. Grace and her team's main mission after being dispatched was to ferry Liberion striker units to Britannia as part of the lend-lease program, and to assist local witches in converting to those models.<ref>[https://twitter.com/humikane/status/1545535787051421696 https://twitter.com/humikane/status/1545535787051421696]</ref>.

After she retired as a witch, she started working on unfamiliar desk work, but based on the belief that songs will save people, she set up a concept of "Aviation Witches Music Squadron" that gathers witches who cannot participate in battles for various reasons. She rushed to get budget and recruit witches.
