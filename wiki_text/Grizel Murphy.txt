'''Grizel J. Murphy''' is a witch originating from [[Britannia]].

==Background==
She is affiliated with the 887th Squadron, the witch unit operating from the Royal Navy's [[Wikipedia:HMS_Indefatigable_(R10)|Indefatigable]] aircraft carrier, which was cooperating with the [[Fuso]] Imperial Navy.

She teamed up with Fuso's witches and shot down two [[Neuroi]].
