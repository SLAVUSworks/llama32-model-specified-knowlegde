'''Gundula Rall''' (グンドュラ・ラル) is a veteran witch of the [[502nd Joint Fighter Wing]], originating from [[Karlsland]], who behind [[Gertrud Barkhorn|Barkhorn]] and [[Erica Hartmann|Hartmann]], is Karlsland Airforce's (and, therefore humanity's) 3rd greatest ace. She achieved 250 kills before Hartmann and Barkhorn, but was later surpassed.

==Background==
An expert at Deflection Shooting, with a certain artistic flair for dogfighting; during the evacuation of [[Karlsland]], however, she was injured by an unlucky shot when her field of view was blocked by a piece of Neuroi debris, resulting in a severely broken back.

Although she was told that it will be impossible to fly again, after pouring her determination into extensive medical treatment with the help of a Viennese healing witch and nine months of rehabilitation she eventually returned to the front. During her hospitalization she was placed adjacent to the future Africa Corps commander [[Katou Keiko]], with whom she gave a popular interview.

To keep her old injuries from flaring up while dogfighting she wears a special corset made of magically woven fibers that reduce G force. It was made by the witch that healed her when she was at the hospital.

Upon her return, the squadron participated in Operation Barbarossa and continued fighting in the direction of Petersburg, where she continued to sortie to not lose to other aces, rapidly increasing the number of kills.

After the operation was abandoned, she was appointed commander of the [[502nd Joint Fighter Wing]], where she demonstrated excellent commanding skills when it was established. When in formation with the [[502nd Joint Fighter Wing|502nd]] her favored tactic is for her to fly with the so-called "Crusher Trio" and leave the practical command decision-making to Flight Lieutenant [[Aleksandra I. Pokryshkin|Pokryshkin]]. This seems to be the best way to handle the diverse group of oddballs.

Within the setting of the Strike Witches' world, she is one of the seven best witches in terms of kill count (the other six being [[Minna-Dietlinde Wilcke|Wilcke]], [[Gertrud Barkhorn|Barkhorn]], [[Heidemarie W. Schnaufer|Schnaufer]], [[Erica Hartmann|Hartmann]], [[Hanna-Justina Marseille|Marseille]] and [[Hanna U. Rudel|Rudel]]).

==Personality==
Strong and confident, Rall takes to her role as commander with relish, acting as more of a brash, frankly-spoken older sister than the more motherly nagging of [[Aleksandra I. Pokryshkin|Pokryshkin]] and nurturing of [[Shimohara Sadako|Shimohara]]. Unlike many in her position, however, she doesn't let herself get too serious, and is laid back and good-humored on a personal level. She has the courage to reassure her surroundings without forgetting to smile, no matter how severe the situation is. She seems to be able to take criticism well, even encouraging in an act of mock masochism from Wilcke when the latter began berating her morals.

She has a sharp eye for talents. She quickly saw Marseille's talent despite being treated as a problem child and vouched for her to be taken more seriously as a pilot. She scouted [[Waltrud Krupinski]], [[Kanno Naoe]] and [[Nikka Edvardine Katajainen|Nikka Katajainen]], the "Crusher Trio" to form the [[502nd Joint Fighter Wing]].

This sharp eye also gives her a reputation for stealing away witches, something both Wilcke and [[Huberta von Bonin|von Bonin]] criticize her for (despite von Bonin doing it herself). Rall is even the reason for the meeting in Helsinki with the aforementioned Karlsland witches, in which she, Wilcke, and von Bonin discuss rules for Joint Fighter Wing recruitment, due to Rall's tendency to use underhanded methods to obtain witches. Rall's cleverness is put to good use within the drama as she predicts the arrival of Erica Hartmann alongside Wilcke, and attempts to use Waltrud Krupinski, who taught Hartmann how to have fun, to recruit her to the 502nd, though this ultimately fails.

==Abilities==
Her inherent magic is called Deflection Shooting (偏差射撃, ''Hensa Shageki''). It's the same as Marseille's skill. There's a possibility that it's a combination of three different magics - [[w:c:powerlisting:Precognition|Future Sight]], [[w:c:powerlisting:Detection|Three Dimensional Space Understanding]] and [[w:c:powerlisting:Bullet Manipulation|Magic Bullet]] - but there are no definite conclusions. This skill allows her to anticipate the target trajectory and shoot where it will go.

==Trivia==
* Her Ace Archetype is [[Wikipedia:Günther Rall|Günther Rall]]. Like Gundula, he was World War II's third highest-scoring ace, with 274 victory claims and one more unconfirmed claim. This number includes one French P-36, one US P-38, and 272 Soviet aircraft on the Eastern Front.
* The Viennese healing witch who treated her is a reference to Hertha Schon, who the real life Rall later married.
