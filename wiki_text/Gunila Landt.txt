'''Gunila Landt''' is a witch originating from [[Karlsland]] and affiliated with the Karlsland Air Force.

==Background==
A young witch assigned to the JG53 in 1943. She was active on the Western Front and destroyed 16 [[Neuroi]] by the end of 1944.
