'''Gunila Specht''' (グーニラ・シュペヒト) is a witch originating from [[Karlsland]] and affiliated with the Karlsland Air Force.

==Background==
A witch famous for her eyepatch just like squadron Leader [[Sakamoto Mio|Sakamoto]].
She has an excellent magic eye and is a perfectionist when it comes to duties. She is also an excellent commander and is famous for making excellent reports, which is why her personal mark is a flying pencil. In addition, her squadron's mark depicting a witch chasing a Neuroi is also famous.

==Trivia==
*Her ace archetype is [[Wikipedia:Günther Specht|Günther Specht]].
