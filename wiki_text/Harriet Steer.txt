'''Harriet Steer''' (ハリエット・スティーア) is a witch originating from [[Britannia]].

==Background==
Harriet was accidentally bumped into by [[Erica Hartmann]] during her and [[Gertrud Barkhorn]]'s
first visit to the port town near the [[501st Joint Fighter Wing|501st]] Britannian Base, during their resupply mission.

She and Erica are both on a resupply mission during the time they met.
They helped a girl named Mary, who was separated from her sister while looking for her cat.
They managed to bring Mary back to the refugee slums, the area she was living in.

==Personality==
Her brightness and kindness that she can share with anyone in difficult situations may have had a positive effect on Erica, who was worried about being a witch.
