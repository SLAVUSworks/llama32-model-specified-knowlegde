'''Heinrike Bär''' is a Witch from [[Karlsland]], and attached with the [[Karlsland|Karlsland Air Force]].

==Background==
She has fought on all [[Karlsland]] war fronts - including the Eastern, Western and Mediterranean - and already achieved countless military results.

Throughout her career, Heinrike took command of two different [[Wikipedia:Wing_(military_aviation_unit)|fighter-wings]], the [[Wikipedia:Jagdgeschwader_1_(World_War_II)#Group_II..2FJG_1|Jagdgeschwader 1 Gruppe II (II./JG 1)]] and the [[Wikipedia:Ergänzungs-Jagdgeschwader#Erg.C3.A4nzungs-Jagdgeschwader_2|Ergänzungs-Jagdgeschwader 2 Gruppe III (III./EJG 2)]]. Of these, the latter is a unit that performs [[Wikipedia:Fleet_Replacement_Squadron|replacement training]] on new models of striker units.
