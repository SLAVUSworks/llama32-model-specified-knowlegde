}}'''Heinrike von Bülow-Bothkamp''' is a Witch originating from [[Karlsland]] and affiliated to the Karlsland Air Force.

==Background==
Heinrike is a witch who has been active since the previous war.

A famous aristocrat, she was asked to coordinate members when establishing the [[506th Joint Fighter Wing]]. She selected Squadron leader [[Edytha Prinzessin zur Lippe-Weissenfeld]] and Flight Lieutenant [[Heinrike Prinzessin zu Sayn-Wittgenstein]] as fighter captain candidates.

==Trivia==
*Her ace archetype is [[Wikipedia:Harry von Bülow-Bothkamp|Harry von Bülow-Bothkamp]]. One of the few people in history who became an ace in both wars.
