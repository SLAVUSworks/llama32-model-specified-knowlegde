'''Helvetia''' (ヘルウェティア<sup>[http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Installing_Japanese_character_sets ?]</sup>, ''heruwetiia''), also known as '''The Helvetia Commonwealth''', is a country in Central Europe between [[Gallia]] and [[Karlsland]]. Its real world counterpart is [[wikipedia:Switzerland|Switzerland]].

The current status of the country is not fully known, but it's presumed to have remained free during the conflict, because Yoshika is invited study in a Helvetian medical school in the [[Strike Witches: The Movie]].

It's exact neutrality is uncertain and questionable, since Strike Witches: Road to Berlin does point out that the nation is in fact a Commonweallth, and military records are stated to note that Helvetia had made purchase of some undetermined number of striker units to bolster military readiness and defense to supplement the defenses that the Alps naturally provide the nation.

== Trivia ==

* Helvetia's real life counterpart, Switzerland, is officially considered to be a Confederation, unlike Helvetia, which is identified as a Commonwealth.
