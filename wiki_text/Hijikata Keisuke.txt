'''Hijikata Keisuke''' (土方 圭助) is the right-hand man to [[Sakamoto Mio|Mio Sakamoto]]. He belongs to the Yokosuka Naval District and holds the rank of Petty Officer (兵曹<sup>[http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Installing_Japanese_character_sets ?]</sup>) in the Fuso Imperial Navy.

==Background==
He is usually seen escorting Sakamoto, but at the beginning of Season 2 he is seen acting as a liaison between Sakamoto, who is partially retired, and the Fuso Imperial Navy.

==Strike Witches "What-if" — 1947==
In Fumikane's "[[Cold Winter 1947|What If - Cold Winter 1947]]" tweets, Hijikata is present alongside Sakamoto during these events.

''This shouldn't be considered entirely canonical, because it was only tweeted by Humikane.''
