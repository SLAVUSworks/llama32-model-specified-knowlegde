=='''Season 1 Hime Uta Albums'''==
Five Hime Uta song albums were made after the first season aired, featuring the witches of the 501st Joint Fighter Wing (and [[Ursula Hartmann|Ursula]]). They each feature several songs sung by the Witches' respective voice actors interspersed with dialogue. [[Sakamoto Mio|Mio]] and [[Miyafuji Yoshika|Yoshika]] star on [[Hime Uta 1 Translation|the first album]]. [[Hime Uta 2 Translation|The second album]] features [[Eila Ilmatar Juutilainen|Eila]] and [[Sanya V. Litvyak|Sanya]] attempting to learn [[Bookmark A Head]] for an upcoming propaganda film. [[Hime Uta 3 Translation|The third album]] stars [[Erica Hartmann|Erica]], [[Gertrud Barkhorn|Trude]] and [[Minna-Dietlinde Wilcke|Minna]]. It also features Ursula Hartmann, who is visiting from Suomus in order to give Sanya the newest version of her Fliegerhammer, which Ursula designed. [[Hime Uta 4 Translation|The fourth album]] sees the return of Yoshika, along [[Lynette Bishop|Lynne]] and [[Perrine H. Clostermann|Perrine]] as they look after the base in anticipation of Mio's return, who is in Fuso helping with casting for an upcoming propaganda film. [[Hime Uta 5 Translation|The fifth album]] stars [[Charlotte E. Yeager|Shirley]] and [[Francesca Lucchini|Lucchini]].
=='''Movie Hime Uta Albums'''==
Five more Hime Uta albums were made after the movie was released on BD/DVD. It features the 501st witches, Hattori Shizuka, Heidemarie Schnaufer, and Fernandia Malvezzi. 4 of 5 volumes have been released, with the last one coming in November.

*Volume 1 - Eila, Sanya, Schnaufer
*Volume 2 - Shirley, Lucchini, Fernandia
*Volume 3 - Barkhorn, Erica, Minna
*Volume 4 - Yoshika, Shizuka, Mio
*Volume 5 - Lynne, Perrine, Amelie Planchard
Based on the dialogue, it seems like the drama CDs occur before the events of the movie.
