The Himegoe CD 2 (秘め声CD弐<sup>[http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Installing_Japanese_character_sets ?]</sup>) Voice Albums are a series of character albums that were bundled with the limited edition DVDs/Blu-rays for the second season. They consist of interviews with the various witches. The various witches list off their background, opinion on the unit and impressions of other characters. Their opinions on other characters can be read on their respective wiki pages. Additionally, they lend their voices to a series of statements that may be used to replace the system sounds on a fan's computer.
