'''The Himegoe Voice Albums''' are a series of character albums that were bundled with the limited edition DVDs for the first season. They consist of interviews with the various members of the 501st. The various members list off their background, opinion on the unit and impressions of other characters. Their opinions on other characters can be read on their respective wiki pages. Additionally, they lend their voices to a series of statements that may be used to replace the system sounds on a fan's computer.
