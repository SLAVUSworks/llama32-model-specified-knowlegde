The [[World Witches media|World Witches]] universe takes place in an alternate universe. Some of the people and events in this universe remains the same as in real-life, but some were subject to change. Also, completely original elements have also been introduced by the author.

It is believed, but not proved, that the existence of Witches heavily influenced in the course of history in this universe. Witches play an important role in history by assisting mankind in fighting [[Neuroi|monsters]] with their magical abilities.

Most of the references used here are from the Strike Witches Fan Book.
