A squadron based on the Isle of Wight in the English Channel. They seem to be in relation with the [[501st Joint Fighter Wing]].

Originally there was no air force base in the Isle of Wight, so their unit's patch is just a modified version of the army base emblem that was there before them. 

The castle in the patch is [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carisbrooke_Castle the Carisbrooke castle].
