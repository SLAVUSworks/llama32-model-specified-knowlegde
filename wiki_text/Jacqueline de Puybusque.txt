'''Jacqueline de Puybusque''' is a witch originating from [[Gallia]]. Her mother was Colonel Puybusque who was active in the previous war.

==Background==
Flying Officer Puybusque is the heir to a duke's family and one of those who was approached to become the commander of the [[506th Joint Fighter Wing]]. She is currently resting in Africa after an injury and is semi-retired.

In addition to being almost incapable of flying due to her injury, the rank of Flying Officer is merely an honorary promotion due to the injury. She is originally a Sergeant Major and has not received formal officer training, so she refuses to be a practical commander.
