'''Jamie E. Swett''' (ジェイミー・E・スウェット) is a Witch of the [[508th Joint Fighter Wing]], originating from [[Liberion]].

==Background==
Because she was a very young witch amongst the Marines her personality is very intense, but she is certainly skillful. By Flight Lieutenant [[Marian Carl|Carl]]'s recommendation, she was sent to the [[508th Joint Fighter Wing|508th JFW]]. Carl did that because she thought it would be good for her to be in a different difficult environment, so she might grow a bit.

Since she used a [[Britannia]]n [[Striker Unit]], Jamie was sent to the aircraft carrier [[Wikipedia:HMS_Victorious_(R38)|Victorious]] to achieve the biggest possible change of environment for her. Even though she is personality-wise completely opposite with Flying Officer [[Delia Jeram|Jeram]], they get along surprisingly well. Because they work well together their military gains are numerous.
