'''Jamie Watt''' is a famous witch known for inventing steam power and more importantly, the Magic Engine in 1765 (according to the timeline in the SW fan book). The inventions helped to pave way for more innovations such as the introduction of the [[Striker Unit]]s in the 20th century.

==Trivia==
*She is based on [[Wikipedia:James Watt|James Watt]].
