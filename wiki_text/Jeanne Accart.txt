'''Jeanne Accart''' is a witch originating from [[Gallia]].

==Background==
A Gallian ace who fought in the evacuation of Gallia and participated in the defense of Karlsland with Ostmark's support.
She retired as a witch after being injured in the withdrawal battle of Gallia. She then chose to become an instructor.
