'''Joanna Elizabeth Stafford''' (ジョアンナ・エリザベス・スタッフォード) is a witch of the [[Music Squadron|League of Nations Air Forces Aviation Magic Band Luminous Witches]], originating from New York, [[Liberion]], and affiliated to the [[Liberion Navy]]. Her nickname is Jo (ジョー, ''Jō''). Her familiar's name is Smile.

==Background==
She is also the eldest daughter of the Stafford family.

When she awakened as a witch, she enlisted in the Liberion Navy in order to support her eight younger brothers in place of her sick parents.

At 12 years old, she is the youngest of the Luminous Witches, and half of the members see her as a little sister.

==Personality==
Jo is dependable, strong-willed, and boyish, having had to take care of her whole family when she was younger. She sometimes speaks very roughly, using the masculine pronoun “ore” due to speaking that way around her brothers. She likes to sew and design clothes, along with Silvie

==Abilities==
She is good at drawing, and is in charge of the design of the Squadron's emblem and everyone's personal mark.
<br>In the unit she is in charge of vocal design.

==Equipment==
The Music Squadron uses the [[wikipedia:North Amercian T-6 Texan variants#AT Series (Texan)|AT-6 Texan]] [[Striker Unit|Striker Units]], a trainer made in [[Liberion]]. This Striker is used in the Britannian Commonwealth as well and nicknamed "Harvard" there<ref>[https://twitter.com/humikane/status/1547058338680020992 Humikane's tweet on the AT-6]</ref>.
