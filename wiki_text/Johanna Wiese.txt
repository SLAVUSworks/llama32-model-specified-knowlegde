'''Johanna Wiese''' (ヨハンナ・ウィーゼ) is a Witch attached with the [[Karlsland|Karlsland Air Force]].

==Background==
A Witch that served as the commanding officer of the [[Wikipedia:Jagdgeschwader_52|Jagdgeschwader 52 Gruppe I (I./JG52)]] around the same time [[Gertrud Barkhorn]] was the commander of Gruppe II.

At one point, she, Barkhorn, [[Erica Hartmann|Hartmann]] and [[Waltrud Krupinski|Krupinski]] had an audience with the Emperor of Karlsland.<ref>http://twitter.com/#!/humikane/status/105178099846627329</ref> If Johanna hadn't been there, [[Wikipedia:Peptic_ulcer|a hole would have opened in Barkhorn's stomach due to stress]] during that small trip.

Johanna was away in instructor duties around the time that Barkhorn was in the [[501st Joint Fighter Wing]], but after the liberation of [[Gallia]] she was promoted to Squadron Leader and got reinstated into the front lines.

==Personality==
A diligent, elder sister-type of character, but she is also a natural airhead and exudes an aura of a seductive female teacher. For [[Gertrud Barkhorn]], Johanna is a precious person as a confidant of the same generation as her.
