'''Kobayashi Sadae''' (小村定恵) is a Witch of the [[508th Joint Fighter Wing]], originating from [[Fuso]] and attached with the [[Fuso Imperial Navy]]. She has a tall physique that is not outdone by the Liberians and a personality that is perpetually largehearted and deeply humane.

==Background==
Since the outbreak of the war, Sadae was on board of the third warship of the European Dispatch Fleet - the [[Wikipedia:Japanese_aircraft_carrier_Shōkaku|Shoukaku]]. It was then arranged for her to be sent together with said mothership to the [[508th Joint Fighter Wing]]. However, the [[Wikipedia:Japanese_aircraft_carrier_Shōkaku|Shoukaku]] got damaged and had to return to Fuso to undergo repairs.

Sadae was supposed to remain on the Shoukaku as always, but because [[Jane Thach|Wing Commander Thach]] from Liberion is a staff officer who rarely fly, she was sent on the [[Wikipedia:USS_Enterprise_(CV-6)|Enterprise]] in an attempt to balance the military strength. This was due to regards that she would not be physically overshadowed by the Liberians and that her personality was also easy-going. This selection turned out to be a success and Sadae soon deepened an unreserved friendship with [[Cecilia Harris|Flight Lieutenant Harris]].
