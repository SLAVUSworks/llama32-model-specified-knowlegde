Kuhaneck is a Sergeant of the [[Liberion]] Military, acts as the Head Mechanic of [[506th Joint Fighter Wing]] B-Unit base and serves as [[Geena Preddy]]'s chief maintenance officer, and has been with her since [[Britannia]].

Preddy used to suffer from a string of incidents that earned her the nickname "Unlucky Preddy". After meeting and hiring Kuhaneck though, Preddy hasn't suffered from a single unlucky incident involving weapon malfunctions or malfunctioning [[Striker Unit|strikers]] like she used to. However, she isn't limited to just maintenance of weapons and strikers: she's also good at housework, makeup, fashion, and media correspondence; and she was apprenticing as a barber before enlisting in the USAAF. 

The reason why photos of Preddy (especially private, intimate photos) are nonexistent is not due to lack of skill on the reporters' part, but rather all thanks to the efforts of Sergeant Kuhaneck, who has been protecting Preddy's privacy with unmatched zeal. It even seems that some reporters have given up on trying to get a scoop at Dijon at all, due to how harshly and effectively Kuhaneck punishes them for their efforts.
