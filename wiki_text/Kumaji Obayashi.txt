The co-pilot of the [[Wikipedia:Kawanishi H8K|Type 2 Flying Boat]] that carried Miyafuji and Sakamoto. His rank is Flying Officer. A flying boat pilot who received intense training in the Yokosuka Air Group along with Flight Lieutenant Hashizume, and is highly trusted by the latter.
