'''Leila L. Shestakov''' (レイラ・L・シェスタコフ) is a witch originating from [[Orussia]]. Her full name is presumed to be "Leila Lvovna Shestakov".

==Background==
She is part of the Orussia nobility, her grandfather being the Admiral of the Navy, who also served as the Minister of the Navy.

A witch with high ability, experience, and command ability who participated in the [[Hispania]] campaign.

When the [[506th Joint Fighter Wing]] was established, a call for dispatch was made by the Orussia side, but this was not realized because her rank was too high to be assigned as a simple member of the unit.

==Trivia==
*Her ace archetype is [[wikipedia:Lev Shestakov|Lev Shestakov]].
