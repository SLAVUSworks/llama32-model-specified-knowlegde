The captain of the [[Wikipedia:Italian battleship Vittorio Veneto|Vittorio Veneto]], the flagship of the Venezian first fleet. His rank is Captain (大佐). Born in a prestigious aristocratic family that produced a large number of Venezian heads of state and Navy commanders in the past, he grew up taking it as a matter of course to live as a Navy soldier. He is considered one of the best captains in the Venezian Navy with his quick and decisive actions and clear mind.
