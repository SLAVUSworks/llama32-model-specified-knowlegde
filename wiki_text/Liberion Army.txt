'''The Liberion Army '''is part of the Liberion Armed Forces of the [[Liberion|United States of Liberion]] in the World Witches. It's very similar to its real world counterpart.

== History==
The Liberion Army was seen alongside the Karlsland Army attacking the Neuroi in 1943 with soldiers and tanks. However, they were completely decimated by the Neuroi when they brought in aerial reinforcements. In late August 1945, the Liberion Army were seen in combat in Belgica when the Neuroi conducted a massive counteroffensive that decimated many of the Liberion Army's positions, one of them being at Bastogne. General Eisenhower would order forces forward to hold the line at Bastogne upon realizing the extent of the Neuroi attack.

Later, General Patton and General Bradley were put in charge of the forces dedicated for Operation Southwind, a military operation to take on the Neuroi Wolf Hive over Berlin. In the beginning of 1946, the Liberion and Karlsland Army landed at Kiel and General Patton initially attempts to drive his forces towards Berlin though a X-34 class Neuroi injured Patton and delays the advance. Later, a X-35 class Neuroi launched an attack on Kiel in which Liberion Calliopes attempted to stop but were unsuccessful and the tanks were destroyed. Finally, at the end of January, the combined Liberion and Karlsland force finally launches an attack on Berlin with General Patton personally leading the attack while General Bradley coordinated from a field base nearby. The Liberion Army provided support with Calliopes which had no effect on the enemy wall types that were made of X-27 class Neuroi. When the Neuroi trapped General Patton's forces in a dome, the Liberion and Karlsland forces took shelter in the Zoo Flak Tower with the wounded being treated by Yoshika. Wolf would be destroyed by the 501st and Liberion and Karlsland officers and soldiers celebrated the liberation of Berlin as the 501st witches flew overhead.
