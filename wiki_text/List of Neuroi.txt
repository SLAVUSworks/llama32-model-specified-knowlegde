== Neuroi Kaii and Rätsel (Monsters, Creatures, and Beasts) ==
Neuroi that existed prior to the events of the Neuroi wars, specifically in that they are were more akin to creatures are listed in this category. This includes any Neuroi that are considered: Kaii and Rätsel, Monsters, Creatures, Beasts, "Supernaturals" or any non-machine based Neuroi referred to as "It".

The time periods of which such Neuroi are typically expected to be found include: The Stone Age, Bronze Age, Iron Age and Classical Antiquity, the Middle Ages, the Sengoku period, the Renaissance and Enlightenment era's, the Industrial/Magic Power Revolution and possible early years of the Great War.

The list of known Neuroi of these kinds and periods are listed as follows:

* Sphinx
*Hydra
*Tsuchigumo yōkai 
*Dragon
