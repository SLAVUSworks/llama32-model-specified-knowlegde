==Witch Units==

===Joint Fighter Wings===
* 501<sup>st</sup> - "[[501st Joint Fighter Wing|Strike Witches]]", Defense of [[Britannia]] and recovery of [[Gallia]] (Later [[Romagna]] and [[Venezia]]).
* 502<sup>nd</sup> - "[[502nd Joint Fighter Wing|Brave Witches]]", Defense and recovery of [[Orussia]] and [[Karlsland]].
* 503<sup>rd</sup> - "[[503rd Joint Fighter Wing|Typhoon Witches]]", Defense and recovery of [[Orussia]] and [[Karlsland]].
* 504<sup>th</sup> - "[[504th Joint Fighter Wing|Ardor Witches]]", General Defense ([[Romagna]]).
* 505<sup>th</sup> - "[[505th Joint Fighter Wing|Mirage Witches]]", General Defense.
* 506<sup>th</sup> - "[[506th Joint Fighter Wing|Noble Witches]]", General Defense (Eastern [[Gallia]]).
* 507<sup>th</sup> - "[[507th Joint Fighter Wing|Silent Witches]]", General Defense ([[Suomus]]).
* 508<sup>th</sup> - "[[508th Joint Fighter Wing|Mighty Witches]]", General Defense (?); An Aircraft Carrier Task Force.

===Joint Fighter Squadrons===
*11<sup>th</sup> - "[[Her Majesty's Witch]]", a.k.a. Glorious Witches; defense of [[Britannia]], especially London.
*31<sup>st</sup> - "[[31st Joint Fighter Squadron Africa|Storm Witches]]", Defense of Africa.
*42<sup>nd</sup> - "Shinigami", Defense of [[Fuso]].
