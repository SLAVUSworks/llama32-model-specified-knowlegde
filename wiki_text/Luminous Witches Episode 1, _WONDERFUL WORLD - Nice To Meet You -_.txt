1944, [[Britannia]]. Mankind has been in a long battle with a mysterious entity called the [[Neuroi]]. [[Virginia Robertson|Ginny]], who travelled all the way to London from the countryside by train, meet two girls named [[Lyudmila Andreyevna Ruslanova|Milasha]] and [[Shibuya Inori|Inori]]. She was surprised to learn that Moffi, whom she carried with her, was a "familiar" that only witches could see. Meanwhile, Squadron Leader [[Grace Maitland Steward|Grace]], who was leading a small music squadron to support everybody menaced by the invasion through the power of music, appealed to the headquarters on the necessity to increase their activities.

== Historical References ==

=== Operation Dynamo ===
[[File:Luminous Witches Operation Dynamo.jpg|thumb]]
The Dunkirk evacuation, codenamed Operation Dynamo by the British, was the evacuation of Allied soldiers from the beaches and harbor of Dunkirk, France, between May 26 and June 4, 1940, when British and French troops were cut off by the German army during the Battle of Dunkirk in the Second World War. Scene was shown in early part was part of evacuation and escorted by Grace Maitland Stewart and her squad.

=== Greenwich Viaduc Railway ===
[[File:Luminous Witches viaduc railway.jpg|thumb]]
The '''London Bridge – Greenwich Railway Viaduct''' consists of a series of nineteen brick railway viaducts linked by road bridges between London Bridge railway station and Deptford Creek, which together make a single structure 3.45 miles (5.55 km) in length. After the title card, there is a scene where Ginny shown inside of train, while the train moving to London via Viaduct. One of the oldest elevated railway and one of the oldest railway in the world.

=== Palace of Westminster ===
[[File:Luminous Witches London house of parliement postcard.png|thumb]]
The Palace of Westminster serves as the meeting place for both the House of Commons and the House of Lords, the two houses of the Parliament of the United Kingdom. Informally known as the Houses of Parliament, the Palace is located on the north bank of the River Thames in the City of Westminster, in central London.

=== Piccadily Circus ===
[[File:Luminous Witches Piccadilly Circus.jpg|thumb]]
'''Piccadilly Circus''' is a road junction and public space of London's West End in the City of Westminster. It was built in 1819 to connect Regent Street with Piccadilly. In this context, a circus, from the Latin word meaning "circle", is a round open space at a street junction. This is the place where Ginny, Inori, and Milasha met by fate.

=== The British Museum ===
The '''British Museum''' is a public museum dedicated to human history, art and culture located in the Bloomsbury area of London. Its permanent collection of eight million works is among the largest and most comprehensive in existence.The British Museum was the first public national museum in the world.


=== Royal Albert Hall & Albert Memorial ===
[[File:Luminous Witches Royal Albert Hall 1.jpg|thumb]]
[[File:Luminous Witches Royal Albert Hall 2.jpg|thumb]]
The '''Royal Albert Hall''' is a concert hall on the northern edge of South Kensington, London. The '''Albert Memorial''', directly north of the Royal Albert Hall in Kensington Gardens, London, was commissioned by Queen Victoria in memory of her beloved husband Prince Albert, who died in 1861. This was the place where Ginny, Milasha, and Inori saw Elie and Ayla for the first time in person, and Albert Memorial was the place where Virginia sang her song.

=== Amazing Grace ===
The song sang by Virginia Robertson on Albert Memorial Monument was based from '''Amazing Grace'''. "'''Amazing Grace'''" is a Christian hymn published in 1779, with words written in 1772 by the English poet and Anglican clergyman John Newton (1725–1807). It is an immensely popular hymn, particularly in the United States, where it is used for both religious and secular purposes. Later this song has a lot of variation and recording, and one of them by Royal Scots Dragoon Guard in 1972. In 1972, the Royal Scots Dragoon Guards, the senior Scottish regiment of the British Army, recorded an instrumental version featuring a bagpipe soloist accompanied by a pipe band. The tempo of their arrangement was slowed to allow for the bagpipes, but it was based on Collins's: it began with a bagpipe solo introduction similar to her lone voice, then it was accompanied by the band of bagpipes and horns, whereas in her version she is backed up by a chorus.

=== USS Santee (CVE-29) ===
[[File:Luminous Witches USS Santee.png|thumb]]
USS Santee was Sangamon-Class Aircraft Carrier that operate in Atlantic Sea. This Carrier used by Liberion to transport Liberion Navy Witches such as Joanna Elizabeth Stafford. 

=== London red buses ===
Buses have been used as a mode of public transport in London since 1829, when George Shillibeer started operating a horse-drawn ''omnibus'' service from Paddington to the City of London. In the decades since their introduction, the red London bus has become a symbol of the city. In World Witches Universe, this buses can be seen in Luminous Witches.

=== Avro Lancaster ===
The '''Avro Lancaster''' is a British Second World War heavy bomber. It was designed and manufactured by Avro as a contemporary of the Handley Page Halifax, both bombers having been developed to the same specification, as well as the Short Stirling, all three aircraft being four-engined heavy bombers adopted by the Royal Air Force (RAF) during the same wartime era. This can be seen on Luminous Witches Opening.

=== Barrage balloons ===
[[File:Luminous witches barrage balloons.png|thumb]]
Large uncrewed tethered balloons used to defend ground targets against aircraft attack, by raising aloft steel cables which pose a severe collision risk to aircraft, making the attacker's approach more difficult.

=== A-11 wristwatch ===
[[File:Luminous Witches A-11 wristwatch.png|thumb]]
This watch model was issued to the US Air Force from 1943 onward. Equipped with a second hand regulator to set the time in seconds.
