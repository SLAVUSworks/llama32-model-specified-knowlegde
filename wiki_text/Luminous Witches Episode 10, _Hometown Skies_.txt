The [[501st Joint Fighter Wing|501st Joint Fighter Wing "Strike Witches"]] destroyed the [[Neuroi]] nest and liberated [[Gallia]]. In the midst of national excitement at this achievement, the first time ever in human history, the Luminous return from the world tour and are warmly welcomed. Their popularity was unimaginable at the time of their world tour debut. They are now a must-have for public relations, and a stage appearance at Gallia liberation commemoration ceremony is also being considered. As a native of Gallia, [[Eleonore Giovanna Gassion|Ellie]] is invited to participate in the field inspection. But behind the scenes, a transfer order for Ginny has been issued...

==Historic References==

===Captioning Ceremony===
Just before students who aim to be nurses and dental hygienists face their first hospital training, faculty members give each student a nurse cap and certify them as nurses and dental hygienists. A ceremony to raise awareness of the occupation as a goal and to make one aware of the weight of the responsibility. Also known as capping. The general content is that a nurse wearing a nurse's cap receives the light from the nightingale statue and recites the nightingale pledge in the light of the candle. Nowadays, with the abolishment of nurse caps, there are schools that do not hold capping ceremonies.

===Meatloaf===
Minced pork or beef, eggs, bread crumbs, onions, seasonings, and other ingredients are mixed and kneaded, put in a mold and baked in the oven. After baking, cut it to your desired thickness from the edge and eat it with your favorite sauce such as tomato sauce, gravy, white sauce, ketchup, or fruit jam.

===Kokuchou===
Endemic species inhabiting Australia, also known as "black swan". It is a roaming bird that does not migrate like the whooper swan and migrates depending on the season and environmental changes. Juvenile birds have white plumage and a black beak, and as they grow, the plumage turns black and the beak turns reddish brown to red. The iris is brown. During the dry season, when the water in the lakes and marshes is low, they search for water and migrate in flocks of several hundred birds. They often fly at night.
