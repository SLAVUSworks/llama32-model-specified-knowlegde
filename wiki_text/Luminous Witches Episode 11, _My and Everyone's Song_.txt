==Historic References==

===Charles de Gaulle Square===
A square in Paris, France. It is famous as a roundabout square with the world-famous Arc de Triomphe de Etoile.

===Étoile Arc de Triomphe===
A triumphal arch located in Place Charles de Gaulle, at the western end of the Champs-Elysées in Paris, France. The Arc de Triomphe is one of the iconic buildings in Paris and all of France, and when people simply say the Arc de Triomphe, they often refer to the Arc de Triomphe de Etoile, making it one of the world's leading tourist attractions.

===King's Cross Station===
One of the main terminal stations in London. Completed in 1852, the station building is a cultural asset designated as a Grade 1 British Designated Building. It is also famous as the starting station of the Hogwarts Express in J.K. Rowling's Harry Potter series.
