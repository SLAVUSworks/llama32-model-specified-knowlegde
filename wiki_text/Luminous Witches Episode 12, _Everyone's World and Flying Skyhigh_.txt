==Historical References==

===Charles de Gaulle===
French politician and soldier. 18th President of the country. After the fall of France by the Nazi invasion in May 1940, he established the Free France of the Cross of Lorraine in London, England, to fight with the Resistance. He became the first Prime Minister of the Provisional Government and in January 1959 became the first President of the Fifth Republic. During his tenure, he recognized the independence of Algeria, armed France with nuclear weapons, and withdrew from NATO's military structure.

===Moulin Rouge===
A performance theater located in Montmartre, Paris, France. Famous for its shows that combine singing, dancing, French cancan and street performances. It continued to operate during World War I and World War II, and even when Paris was occupied by the German army during World War II, it was crowded with many German soldiers. Elvis Presley, Frank Sinatra, and many other famous musicians have worked here.

===Parabolic Antenna===
A concave antenna with a parabolic reflector. It is mainly used for multiplex radio communications, satellite communications, satellite broadcasting, and radio astronomy.
