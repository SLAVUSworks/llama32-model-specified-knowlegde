== References ==

=== Shinmachi Boardwalk ===
A boardwalk (wooden promenade) set up along the banks of the Shinmachi River, which flows through the center of Tokushima City, Tokushima Prefecture. There is the main stage of the anime event "Machi Asobi" held twice a year. Luminous Witches also performed live in 2019.

=== Hibiya Open Air Concert Hall ===
An open air concert hall in Hibiya Park in Chiyoda Ward, Tokyo. Established in July 1923. Temporarily closed from 1943 during the Pacific War. After the war, it was requisitioned by GHQ, but after requisition was lifted in August 1954, it was renovated and reopened as the second large music hall. As the building began to deteriorate, it underwent a complete renovation in 1982, and was completed in August 1983 as the third grand concert hall.

=== Non-Commissioned Officer ===
One of the military rank divisions. Although it differs depending on the country, it generally refers to a sergeant major or a sergeant who manages general soldiers.

=== T-6 Texan ===
The T-6 Texan (Texan) was used from the 1930s to the 1960s. A reciprocating advanced training aircraft manufactured by North American. It was used by the United States Army and Navy, as well as by the United Kingdom and Commonwealth countries, and was used by many more countries, including Japan, after World War II. It was called AT-6 in the US Army Air Corps, SNJ in the US Navy, and "Harvard" in the Commonwealth countries.

=== Heston Airport ===
It was operated from 1929 to 1947 at an airport located west of London, England. In September 1938 British Prime Minister Chamberlain flew from Heston Airfield to Germany three times in two weeks for a meeting with Hitler. He returned from the Munich Conference at Heston Airfield where he delivered his famous speech '''"I believe it is peace for our time"''' . However, less than a year after the Munich Conference, Nazi Germany invaded Poland and World War II began. It has become a symbol of what is not.
