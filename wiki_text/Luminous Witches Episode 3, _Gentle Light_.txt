=== Haka (dance) ===
This is New Zealand's Maori folk dance. Danced mainly by men. Originally, Maori warriors clapped their hands and stomped their feet before battle to show off their strength and provoke their opponents. Today, it is performed as a welcome dance for state guests and travelers from overseas, and is famous as a folk dance performed by the New Zealand rugby team (All Blacks) before international matches.
