=== May Festival ===
[[File:1-4 Maypole.png|thumb]]
A festival originating from an ancient Roman festival. On May 1st, offerings were made to Maia, the goddess of fertility. Today, it has become established in various parts of Europe as a day to celebrate the arrival of spring, which dates back to pre-Christian origins.


===Maypole Dance===
[[File:1-4 Maypole Dance (1).png|thumb]]
The custom of setting up pillars at festivals is seen all over the world. In Europe, Maypoles are set up around churches and town squares during the May Festival, a festival that welcomes May (spring) dance. An event to express gratitude for the blessings of nature that has been handed down from ancient times in European countries.
