The Luminous Witches, who made their musical performance debut on stage in [[Romagna]], are staying in Greece during transit, which also serves as holidays. [[Maria Magdalene Dietrich|Maria]], aiming to further improve their live performance, propose to incorporate shields to the flight program. However, Maria insist to practice on the ground, while [[Manaia Matawhaura Hato|Mana]] wants to fly around with [[Striker Unit|Striker Units]]. This leads to a quarrel between the two. Maria has high expectations. But this is the kind of conflict in which no one manage to be honest with their feelings...

== References ==

=== Castle Nuovo in Naples ===
An ancient medieval castle in Naples, Italy. The royal palace was built in 1279 and was the residence of successive kings of Naples. It is now one of the city's major architectural landmarks.

=== Madrid's Retiro Park ===
A park with a vast site adjacent to the Prado Museum in Madrid. There is big pond in the center of park, and monument of Madrid Alfonso XII is built.

=== Cityscape of Algiers and Carthaginian ruins in Tunisia ===
The ancient city of Carthage was built by the Phoenicians of the maritime people in the 9th century BC. After conquering the Mediterranean, it was defeated by ancient Rome, and most of the city was destroyed, but was later rebuilt by the Romans. Designated as a World Heritage Site in 1979.
