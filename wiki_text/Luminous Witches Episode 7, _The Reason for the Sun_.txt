After performing in North Africa, the next venue is in [[Orussia]], [[Lyudmila Andreyevna Ruslanova|Milasha]]'s homeland. However, something is wrong with Moffy since the move to Orussia. After hearing that there is someone very knowledgeable about familiars living near the airport, [[Virginia Robertson|Ginny]] and the others head there immediately. And thus, they meet a gentle woman named Anna, and learn that the nearby town is being built by villagers who escaped from a [[Neuroi]] attack and evacuated their home town. These people are working tirelessly to rebuild their lives. The Luminous scramble and use their powers as much as possible.

==Trivia==
*This episode takes place in parallel with [[Strike Witches]] 6th episode called [[Season 01 Episode 06, "Solidarity"|Solidarity]]. Unaware to Sanya, [[Miyafuji Yoshika|Yoshika]], and [[Eila Ilmatar Juutilainen|Eila]] at the time, the [[サーニャのうた (Sanya no Uta)|piano solo]] sent to Sanya from her father through radio broadcasting was influenced and aided by the Luminous Witches.
*This is the first anime appearance of the [[503rd Joint Fighter Wing]], and [[Sanya V. Litvyak|Sanya]]'s parents.
*In the episode portrait, Ginny is seen holding a broken piano key with, but behind her in the sky are night witch waves shaped like Sanya's magic antenna, so spiritually the portrait represents the two night witches.
