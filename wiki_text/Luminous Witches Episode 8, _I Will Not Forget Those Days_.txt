After finishing their live performance in the [[Fuso]] Empire, the Luminous Witches went to [[Shibuya Inori|Inori]]'s grandmother's house. Everyone is enthusiastic about cooking, cleaning, and arranging their own accommodation. But [[Lyudmila Andreyevna Ruslanova|Milasha]] was the only one to make a mistake at a crucial moment and to fall behind, while [[Eleonore Giovanna Gassion|Ellie]] is praised by [[Aira Paivikki Linnamaa|Aira]]. This then sparked her rivalry against Ellie, and she challenged her in a one-on-one contest.

=== Hibiya Open-air Concert Hall ===
An open-air concert hall located in Hibiya Park in Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo. Established in July 1923. Temporarily closed from 1943 during the Pacific War. After the war, it was requisitioned by GHQ, but after requisition was lifted in August 1954, it was renovated and reopened as the second large music hall. As the building began to deteriorate, it underwent a complete renovation in 1982, and was completed in August 1983 as the third grand concert hall.
