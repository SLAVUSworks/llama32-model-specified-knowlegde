==Historic References==

===Broadway (New York)===
A main street running north-south in the state of New York, United States. Among them, the section running through Manhattan, New York City is particularly famous. The word "Broadway" has become synonymous with "musical" because it runs through Manhattan's downtown from north to south, and the theater district spreads around Times Square.

===Governors Island===
An island in New York Bay. The U.S. National Park Service maintains a small section of the northern part of the island as a National Monument, containing two former military forts named Fort Jay and Castle Williams.

===Fort Jay===
Fort Jay is a coastal fortress built on Governors Island and a former US Army garrison. It is the island's oldest surviving defensive facility and is named after Federalist member John Jay, Governor of New York, Chief Justice of the Supreme Court, Secretary of State, and one of the Founding Fathers of the United States.

===Castle Williams===
A star-shaped fortress is a castle-building method that occurred in Italy after the middle of the 15th century in order to deal with artillery. The circular fortifications of the Middle Ages with high vertical walls became vulnerable after the spread of artillery. A star fortress, on the other hand, has many bastions (triangular peaks), each designed to cover one another.

===Skyscrapers in the United States===
Skyscrapers began to be built one after another in the Manhattan area of ​​New York, eventually forming a group of skyscrapers, which are called "skyscrapers" and symbolize the country. It will be one of the landscapes to do.

===Brooklyn Bridge===
A bridge connecting Manhattan and Brooklyn across the East River in New York City, USA. It is one of the oldest suspension bridges in the United States and also the first suspension bridge in the world to use steel wires. It took 14 years to build and complete in 1883. It was listed as a National Historic Landmark in 1964 and as a Civil Engineering Historic Landmark in 1972.
