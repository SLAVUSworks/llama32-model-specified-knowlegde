'''Lyudmila Andreyevna Ruslanova''' (リュドミラ・アンドレエヴナ・ルスラノヴァ) is a witch of the [[Music Squadron|League of Nations Air Forces Aviation Magic Band Luminous Witches]], originating from [[Orussia]] and affiliated with the Orussia Imperial Army. Her nickname is Milasha. Her familiar's name is Olivier.

==Background==

==Personality==
A girl with a personality of extremely energetic highs and lows, but who is always at the mercy of her surroundings. Although she has no intention of doing so, her natural energy and caring personality combine to make those around her wonder why she is always angry. The [[Virginia Robertson|protagonist]]'s rival whose fashion sense is a bit lame. She secretly adds poems to her flight log.

==Abilities==
Within the unit, she is in charge of vocals and lyrics.

==Equipment==
The Music Squadron uses the [[wikipedia:North Amercian T-6 Texan variants#AT Series (Texan)|AT-6 Texan]] [[Striker Unit|Striker Units]], a trainer made in [[Liberion]]. This Striker is used in the Britannian Commonwealth as well and nicknamed "Harvard" there<ref>[https://twitter.com/humikane/status/1547058338680020992 Humikane's tweet on the AT-6]</ref>.
