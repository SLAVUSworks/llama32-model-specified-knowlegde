'''Manaia Matawhaura Hato''' (マナイア・マタワウラ・ハト) is a witch of the [[Music Squadron|League of Nations Air Forces Aviation Magic Band Luminous Witches]], originating from [[New Zeiland]] and affiliated with the New Zealand Air Force. Her nickname is Mana (マナ). Her familiar's name is Kyuu-chan.

==Background==
Eats well, sleeps well, and is growing up fast, she is one of the most energetic witches in the NZ Air Force. She loves to exercise and can be found dancing whenever she has spare time. Kiwis are said to be unsuitable as a witch's familiar due to their capricious nature, but she calls it "'''Kyu-chan'''♥" (キューちゃん) due to its cuteness.

==Personality==
Opposite to [[Maria Magdalene Dietrich]], Mana is an energetic and cheerful girl who is always optimistic. She likes to move all the time, but doesn't like being still.

==Abilities==
Within the unit, she is in charge of vocals and dance choreography.

==Equipment==
The Music Squadron uses the [[wikipedia:North Amercian T-6 Texan variants#AT Series (Texan)|AT-6 Texan]] [[Striker Unit|Striker Units]], a trainer made in [[Liberion]]. This Striker is used in the Britannian Commonwealth as well and nicknamed "Harvard" there<ref>[https://twitter.com/humikane/status/1547058338680020992 Humikane's tweet on the AT-6]</ref>. When the AT-6 were delivered to the Music Squadron, Mana said "The Harvard, how nostalgic", indicating that she might already be familiar with the type, probably because of her military training in the Britannian Commonwealth.

==Trivia==
*Her archetype is [[Wikipedia:Ana Hato|Ana Matawhaura Hato]].
*Due to her archetype's inspiration and the appearance of her character design and background; she is a [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M%C4%81ori_people Māori]. This also means she is one of the first confirmed witches that can be considered as being of "indigenous" demography origins.
*Her familiar, being a kiwi; is a bird species recognized as an icon of New Zealand, and may hold similar national significance for New Zeiland.
