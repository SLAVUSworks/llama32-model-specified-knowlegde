'''Maria Magdalene Dietrich''' (マリア・マグダレーネ・ディートリヒ) is a witch of the [[Music Squadron|League of Nations Air Forces Aviation Magic Band Luminous Witches]], originating from [[Karlsland]]. Her familiar's name is Flocke.

==Background==
She hails from [[Karlsland]], a country that prides itself on rigid order and its strong military tradition. While she is not part of the Night Witches, her familiar is nocturnal, and she has excellent night vision. However, she has some trouble getting up in the morning.

Prior to joining the Luminous Witches, she was deployed to the African theatre where she saved "some foxy guy" who was lost in the desert and was promoted to her current rank because of it.

==Personality==
She is very soft spoken but retains a formality one would expect out of a Karlslander witch. She has an inability to wake up on time for morning duties and can usually be seen yawning or trying to nap when possible. However, when it comes to creating aerobatic maneuvers for the group, she is very meticulous and strives for perfection during rehearsals.

She suffers from imposter syndrome. This is seen more clearly during a spat with fellow member [[Manaia Matawhaura Hato|Mana]]. During this, she admits that though she is able to come up with complex maneuvers, she feels that she herself is too weak to attempt them. She also says that her rank was nothing but a windfall and that she feels she did not deserve it. 

==Abilities==
Within the unit, she is in charge of vocals and acrobatics.

==Equipment==
The Music Squadron uses the [[wikipedia:North Amercian T-6 Texan variants#AT Series (Texan)|AT-6 Texan]] [[Striker Unit|Striker Units]], a trainer made in [[Liberion]]. This Striker is used in the Britannian Commonwealth as well and nicknamed "Harvard" there<ref>[https://twitter.com/humikane/status/1547058338680020992 Humikane's tweet on the AT-6]</ref>.
