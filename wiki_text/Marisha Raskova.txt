'''Marisha Raskova''' (Мариша Раскова) is a Witch originating from [[Orussia]].

==Background==
She is considered a legendary witch in Orussia. She liked to listen to [[Sanya V. Litvyak]]'s music at the time she was playing piano in Vienna with her father.
