'''Matsuda Shouko''' (松田昌子) is a Witch of the [[508th Joint Fighter Wing]], originating from [[Fuso]] and attached with the [[Fuso Imperial Navy]].

==Background==
Shouko is a youngling who joined the military after the Neuroi invasion, but her skills are top-class even in the Fuso Navy and she has accomplished the shot-down of countless Neuroi.

Originally, Shouko was attached to the same unit as [[Karibuchi Takami|Flight Lieutenant Karibuchi]] and was in the middle of training to master the [[Wikipedia:Kawanishi_N1K|Shiden Model 11]].

Her sharp air battle skills - that are just like a honed [[Wikipedia:Japanese_sword|Fuso sword]] - and tactics of directly rushing towards the enemy almost without looking back caused those who saw her to feel something reckless in them. Sensing danger in that fighting style, Flight Lieutenant Karibuchi thought that a change of environments would allow her to keep some composure and recommended Shouko to the [[508th Joint Fighter Wing]].

After being assigned to the 508th JFW, Shouko now carries out two-man cell [[Wikipedia:Echelon_formation|rotte tactics]]. Furthermore, as she assumed the duties of protecting her leading fighter as a wingman, her reckless behavior has all but disappeared.
