'''Maxi Immelmann''' is a witch originating from [[Karlsland]].

==Background==
Maxi is a witch from the first [[Neuroi]] war. She was a subordinate of [[Oswalda Boelcke]].

She invented the air combat technique called "[[Wikipedia:Immelmann turn|Immelmann turn]]".

==Trivia==
*Her ace archetype is [[Wikipedia:Max Immelmann|Max Immelmann]]. He was the first WW1 German ace, and commonly known as "Eagle of Reel".
