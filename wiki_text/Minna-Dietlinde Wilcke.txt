'''Minna Dietlinde Wilcke''' (ミーナ・ディートリンデ・ヴィルケ) is a veteran witch of the [[501st Joint Fighter Wing]] originating from [[Karlsland]] and attached with the [[Karlsland|Karlsland Air Force]]. She became renowned as the Ace of Spades (スペードのエース, ''Supēdo no Ēsu''), although some also call her by the nickname of [[Wikipedia:Fürst|Fürstin]] (フュルスティン, ''Fyurusutin''), meaning 'Princess'. Her call sign is Pik-Ass.

==Background==
Minna was born in [[Wikipedia:Poznań|Posnania]], located in eastern [[Karlsland]], near the national border with [[Ostmark]]. One theory says that the Wilcke family are the descendants of [[Wikipedia:Anna Magdalena Bach|Anna Wilcke]], a singer who was also the second wife of [[Wikipedia:Johann Sebastian Bach|Bach]], and because of that they thrive at music, but it is merely no more than a rumour.

Minna is a talented singer; in her youth she aspired to study music abroad at a conservatory in [[Wikipedia:Vienna|Wien]] or at the [[Wikipedia:Mozarteum University of Salzburg|Mozarteum]] in [[Wikipedia:Salzburg|Salzburg]], but had to give up on it on account of the [[Neuroi]] invasion. She sang [[Wikipedia:Lili Marleen|Lili Marleen]] as a farewell gift for the crew of the departing [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_aircraft_carrier_Akagi aircraft carrier Akagi].

Minna often sang along to the piano of [[Kurt Flachfeld]], a young man who lived in a neighboring house in her town who she adored like an older brother. Kurt would later enlist in the army and apply to the maintenance troops in order to follow her into the battlefield. Ever since she lost him in the [[Wikipedia:Nord-Pas de Calais|Pas de Calais]] evacuation, Minna seems to have a tendency of losing her composure when a decision concerning the life and death of a specially close person is demanded from her and still has issues struggling with her grief to this day.

When the Neuroi first invaded, Minna was a Flying Officer serving as the commander of the [[Wikipedia:Jagdgeschwader 53|Jagdgeschwader 53 (JG 53) 7th Staffel]]. Then, during the Karlsland evacuation, she was promoted to Flight Lieutenant and became the leader of the Gruppe III of that same unit. It was also around that time that she deepened her friendship with [[Gertrud Barkhorn|Flight Lieutenant Barkhorn]] and [[Erica Hartmann|Flying Officer Hartmann]]. Later, when the [[Wikipedia:Jagdgeschwader 3|Jagdgeschwader 3 (JG3)]] was undergoing a reorganization in [[Gallia]], she was appointed as its commanding officer.

After going over to [[Britannia]], Minna proposed the establishment of a Joint Fighter Wing to the top brass. After doing things like selecting the members together with Squadron Leader [[Sakamoto Mio|Sakamoto]], she established the [[501st Joint Fighter Wing]] with the backing of [[Adolfine Galland|Air Commodore Galland]] and [[Wikipedia:Hugh Dowding, 1st Baron Dowding|Air Chief Marshall Dowding]]. After the liberation of [[Gallia]] and the disbandment of the 501st JFW, Minna moved to [[wikipedia:Sint-Truiden Air Base|St. Trond Airbase]], [[Belgica]], along with Flight Lieutenant Barkhorn and Flying Officer Hartmann, engaging in Karlsland recapture operations.

Ever since the 501st JFW was reformed in [[Romagna]], the time she spent doing desk work became overwhelmingly longer than her time sortieing, and her shoot-down count got stuck on 199 units for a while. She later single-handedly crushed an ultra-small Neuroi that trespassed into the base by herself and was awarded with the [[Wikipedia:Knight's Cross of the Iron Cross#Knight.27s Cross with Oak Leaves and Swords .28mit Eichenlaub und Schwertern.29|Ritterkreuz des Eisernen Kreuzes mit Eichenlaub und Schwertern]] in praise to her 200th shot-down. This exploit was reported in a grand scale as the embodiment of the Karlsland spirit, to the point that the slogan "''Follow Wing Commander Minna!''" was propagated for some time. Within the setting of the Strike Witches' world, she one of the best-seven witches in terms of shot down count (the other six being [[Erica Hartmann|Hartmann]] , [[Gertrud Barkhorn|Barkhorn]] , [[Heidemarie W. Schnaufer|Schnaufer]] , [[Gundula Rall|Rall]] , [[Hanna-Justina Marseille|Marseille]] and [[Hanna U. Rudel|Rudel]]).

==Personality==
She is skilled at leading operations and squad management and, rather than pursuing her own individual military gains, she focuses on the efficient military performance of the squad as a whole. She also has a tendency to look after her subordinates, which causes some to have a motherly impression of her.

Perhaps due to coming from a family that produced musicians for generations, Minna's demeanor is graceful and gentle, overflowing with elegance. She rarely gets angry, but possesses the backbone to stand in direct opposition in face of unreasonableness no matter who the adversary is. Although blessed with resourcefulness and flexible thinking, she is fragile once the last line in her heart is crossed and also has an inflexible side that relies on rules and structure to maintain her mental balance. If peace happens to come, her dream for the future is to become a singer.

Minna has been portrayed as caring and having a strong partnership with [[Sakamoto Mio|Squadron Leader Sakamoto]], a comrade in arms from since before the [[501st Joint Fighter Wing]]'s formation who often works together with her from a squad management standpoint. This is to the point that she worried about the relationship that Sakamoto, who was then working as an instructor at a training school in Yokosuka, had with her students. Also, because Squadron Leader Sakamoto and [[Miyafuji Yoshika|Sergeant Miyafuji]] from her unit often displayed extremely unprecedented behavior, she tends to view Fuso Witches under a mild veneer of resignation in general.

She's also quite close with Flight Lieutenant Barkhorn and Flying Officer Hartmann. She has known them since the beginning of the war, especially Barkhorn, and considers them both as her best friends. She acts like a mother to the rookies, like [[Miyafuji Yoshika]], [[Lynette Bishop]] and [[Francesca Lucchini]].

As someone who prefers showers, Minna is very self-conscious and has a hard time dealing with the Fuso-style public baths, which several people use at once. On that note, it seems that she deliberately chooses a time when she can bathe alone. She also seems to like food and drink with an extremely strong taste.

==Abilities==
[[File:Minna Spatial Awareness Magic POV.png|thumb|How Minna's Three Dimensional Space Understanding (aka Spatial Awareness) works from Minna's perspective.]]
Minna's inherent magic is called [[w:c:powerlisting:Detection|Three Dimensional Space Understanding Ability]] (三次元空間把握能力, ''Sanjigen Kūkan Haaku Nōryoku''). Classified in the perception-lineage, it makes possible to perceive and discern all nearby targets in every direction, sorting out their position, number and classification. It also serves her well as a commander, being useful for noticing small details about others and maintaining unit cohesion (such as appearing suddenly to diffuse arguments). This ability assisted her in performing air-to-air bombing missions. Also thanks to the power of her inherent magic, she can make her subordinates sortie from an aircraft carrier and then reliably bring them back even from a place above the ocean with no landmarks.

The exact way her Inherent magic works is, when activated, Minna is able to visualize the world around her split into a three dimensional grid map of arbitrary size, indicating a general position, placement and location of the unit inside the grid, and thus the space around her. This allows her to detect a unit, whether a friendly or an enemy; inside the specific grid space sector in question relative to her position in the area to some degree and than provide information to her allies relating to the unit's grid sector location, unit type, intent and other active intel, although exact location may not be exact to the minute measurement and may only indicate exact grid positioning in the three dimensional map. It's unclear if Minna's magic has any passive capabilities or can be used passively, but the likelihood of passive use in this manner is at least questionable and not yet certain. It seems that clouds, walls and other obstructions or concealment that may provide obstruction visually appears to not actually have any affect in perceiving a target in Minna's perceived grid map sector or any other general information, although it's possible that exact pinpointing of a target exactly in the grid sector may require effort on Minna's part, or that of her subordinates, to actually visually and physically inspect and find the target. And indication of the targets exact typing and class through use of the Magical ability seems to be somewhat unclear if it's a unit that is not known prior. 

Minna is also very skilled in battle, and her leadership in combat is very good. The care she shows to her Captains, and the attributes necessary for a Commander, she has everything that a unit leader needs. On top of that, she is an excellent soldier in an air battle.

* Her ace archetype is [[Wikipedia:Wolf-Dietrich Wilcke|Wolf-Dietrich Wilcke]]. Similar to Minna, Wolf-Dietrich bore the nickname "Fürst" amongst his friends and squadmates.
* Her familiar is a Gray Wolf called Krawatte<ref>[https://twitter.com/humikane/status/9158174924 知らなくても害のないSW知識]</ref><ref>[https://twitter.com/humikane/status/568765040586723328 familiar name explaination from Humikane]</ref>, possibly in reference to her archetype's name, Wolf.

*In Road to Berlin: Minna's name in the subtitles is misspelled as "Mina".
*In Road to Berlin, Minna's MG42 has a firing sound effect indicating a lower rate of fire, hinting that Minna's MG42 is using a heavy bolt in her MG42 for a much lower rate of fire for better ammo conservation and control. Gertrud's duel wielded MG42's also shares this attribute.
