'''Miyafuji Sayaka''' (宮藤清佳) is [[Akimoto Yoshiko]]'s daughter, [[Miyafuji Ichiro]]'s wife, [[Miyafuji Yoshika|Yoshika's]] mother, and the apparent owner of the [[Miyafuji Clinic]] in [[Fuso]].

Sayaka's inherent magic is [[w:c:powerlisting:Healing|Healing Abilities]] (治癒能力, Chiyu Nōryoku). Classified as a variety of the telekinesis-lineage, it puts a living thing back to how it was by means of magic and is effective not only against external wounds but also diseases.
