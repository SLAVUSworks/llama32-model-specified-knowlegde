'''Mori Ranmaru''' is a witch based on [[Wikipedia:Mori_Ranmaru|Mori Ranmaru]], a page and vassal of [[Wikipedia:Oda_Nobunaga|Oda Nobunaga]].

==Background==
Mori Ranmaru served Oda Nobunaga when he, [[Wikipedia:Toyotomi_Hideyoshi|Toyotomi Hideyoshi]], and [[Wikipedia:Tokugawa_Ieyasu|Tokugawa Ieyasu]] were battling to unify Fuso.

During the [[Wikipedia:Honnō-ji_Incident|Honno-ji Incident]], when [[Wikipedia:Akechi_Mitsuhide|Akechi Mitsuhide]] attempted to betray and kill Nobunaga; Mori Ranmaru was instrumental in preventing her Lord's death. Though flying was difficult enough alone; she managed to fly Nobunaga out of Honno-ji, and eventually across a great distance until the two arrived at [[Wikipedia:Takamatsu_Castle_(Bitchu)|Takamatsu Castle]], where Nobunaga's ally Toyotomi Hideyoshi was involved in a siege.

In the literature from the time, it is commonly recounted that Mori Ranmaru eventually became Oda Nobunaga's lover.

Mori would follow Nobunaga as he went on to become known as a reformer whose actions and policies lead to the opening of Fuso to the outside world.

According to [[Inagaki Mami]] in [[Africa Doujinshi#Witches of the Sphinx 3|Witches of the Sphinx 3]], Mori Ranmaru is a relation from the Sengoku Period on her mother's side.

== Trivia ==

* The pair of Cannons that Mori Ranmaru used during the Siege of Honoji appear to be purpose-built handcannons called the [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%8Cdzutsu Ōdzutsu]. Ōdzutsu was meant to be used in naval and siege battles, and was highly efficient in destroying large enemy structures. In the Sengoku witches media: she uses them as portable heavy artillery against foot soldiers.
