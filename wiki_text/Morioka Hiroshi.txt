'''Morioka Hiroshi''' (森岡寛) is a Witch from [[Fuso]] and attached with the [[Fuso Imperial Navy]].

==Background==
Flight Lieutenant Morioka was enlisted as a dive-bombing witch after completing the officer training, and was so talented that she was employed as an instructor despite her young age. She was transferred to the air combat witches and joined the 302nd Air Group. She became a particularly young Flight Commanding Officer of the  [[Fuso Imperial Navy|Navy]] Air Corps.
