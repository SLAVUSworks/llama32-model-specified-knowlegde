'''Nakajima Kodaka''' (中島小鷹) is a Witch from [[Fuso]] and attached with the [[Fuso Imperial Army]]. She is the older sister of [[Nakajima Nishiki]] and [[Nakajima Hayate]].

==Trivia==
*Kodaka does not have an ace archetype. “Kodaka” is a collective term for small falcons, so there is a high possibility that she is based on the [[Wikipedia:Nakajima_Ki-43|Nakajima Ki-43 Hayabusa]] (peregrine falcon) fighter.
