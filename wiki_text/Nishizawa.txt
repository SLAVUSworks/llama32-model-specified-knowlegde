'''Nishizawa''' (西澤) is a Witch from [[Fuso]] and attached with the [[Fuso Imperial Army]]. She was a side character in [[Tenkū no Otome-tachi|Strike Witches: Maidens in the Sky]]. '''This manga has since been retconned, and no longer considered canon.'''

==Background==
She is first seen training at the Yokosuka base under the guidance of [[Takei Junko]]. She is one of the three combat-ready witches at the base, aside from Junko.

==Trivia==
*Her ace archetype was believed to be [[Wikipedia:Hiroyoshi_Nishizawa|Hiroyoshi Nishizawa]], but this pilot was eventually used for [[Nishizawa Yoshiko]] instead after [[Tenkū no Otome-tachi|Maidens in the Sky]] was retconned by Humikane.
